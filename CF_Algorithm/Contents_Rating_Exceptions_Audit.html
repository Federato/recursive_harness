1. Specific Rating RatingType ≠ "Class"

Manual grounding: Rule 34, "Specific and Blanket Insurance," §A (p.34) — "Insurance is specific when the entire amount of insurance under a particular item applies at one location to one type of property." Every exception in this section fires when a risk is entered as Specific (or Tentative) rated instead of Class rated — a real, filed alternative to the class-code lookups the live rater is built around, not an edge case invented by the rule engine.

2. Blanket Rating IncludedInBlkt = "Yes"

Manual grounding: Rule 34, "Specific and Blanket Insurance," §B (p.34) — Blanket Insurance covers under one amount either one type of property across more than one separately rated building, or two-plus property types in one or more buildings. The ERC rule mechanics (which Blkt* field to copy) aren't spelled out in this manual passage — that's rate-manual/ERC territory — but the underlying concept is confirmed filed.
