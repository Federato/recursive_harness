CF_Algorithm · whole Property line · the five core coverages

One coverage, four ways to price it — where each rating path stands today

The categorized grid shows which coverage families exist. This page answers a different question for the five core coverages themselves — Building, Personal Property, Special Class, Business Income, and Special Class Business Income — how does each one actually get rated, once you've picked a cause-of-loss form? ISO's own rule files branch into up to four distinct premium paths per coverage per form: a primary scheduled rate (the everyday per-item rating), a Specific/Tentative scheduled variant (Basic Group I only), a Legal Liability premium, and a blanket-rated roll-up across locations.
