Switched on at the location, priced on each coverage's own flood record

Flood is an endorsement, not a cause of loss. The location sets FloodCovIndicator "Yes" and carries a General Information record: the flood zone (A, X, X500 or D), an optional Community Rating System grade, and the deductible. Each coverage then has its own flood record with a limit, and prices it: the flood loss cost (by zone and construction) x the loss cost multiplier x an aggregate factor x a deductible-table factor or an excess-over-NFIP factor, x the CRS credit, then the policy modifiers. The deductible picks one of seven rate-modification tables. The two Business Income coverages use the BI factor instead of the deductible, excess and CRS machinery.

Where the loss cost comes from depends on the state: 37 states file a state table, 7 use county tables with an all-other fallback, and AK and FL file none and price a manual premium. Flood has its own blanket, whose premium sits on the blanket detail, not the members. The policy total moves by exactly the flood premiums. Probes ran in OH (no flood overrides), VA and TX (county tables), OR, MA, NY, TN, NV, UT, MO, AK, FL and DC.

No General Information
$0, no message

With the indicator "Yes" but no General Information record, an empty one is attached and every flood record prices $0 without a message.

Operational unit (OI-122)
Order-dependent

The op-unit BI flood rate never stores its maximum: $0 with a CRS grade, otherwise the last building's rate ($1,434 Frame-then-JM, $1,935 reversed). ISO does the same.

The 46-state sweep
Blanket $0

Chunk 25's 46-state sweep agreed with ISO, but every blanket in it priced $0 (limit above values). Priced blankets were ISO-checked only in OR, IL, NC, MO and FL.

What to notice

  • The flood zone is a loss-cost key, not a factor. Only the data values A / X / X500 / D have rows; sending the NFIP display value "AE" refuses.
  • The countrywide loss-cost table is header-only in every edition, so a missing state row is a refusal (DC files no zone D).
  • Insurance to value above 100% prices $0 on the property records; a limit $1 over rounds to 100% and still prices.
No flood input refuses for being blank. No flood field is in the C28-17 list, so a blank zone, deductible, construction, excess flag, coinsurance, IncludedInBlkt or missing General Information all price $0 with outcome "rated". The only flood refusals are table misses: an unfiled zone or construction, and Business Income's blank type of risk.

What to notice

  • Flood is switched on at the location, but the coverages' flood records are never removed: with the indicator "No" or blank they still run, price $0 and each raises "General Information needs to be attached".
  • With the indicator "Yes" and no General Information sent, an empty record is attached and every flood record prices $0 with no message at all.
  • The deductible chooses one of seven tables (500 / 1,000 → table 1 … 1,000,000 → table 7), read at the coverage's own insurance-to-value; a blank or unlisted deductible gives factor 0.
  • The CRS credit applies only when a grade is entered; "10", "Unknown" and "Not Applicable" mean 1.0.
A blank VA county and city is not $0: it rates on the all-other table ($1,480 / $2,543). Tables 5–7 file factor 0 where the deductible nears the value; that is a filed zero (rated, with two "deductible ≥ value" messages), not a missing row.

What to notice

  • The building always takes the location's deductible, even in a blanket. OH happy path $1,335 on table 3.
  • "Excess over NFIP" swaps the deductible table for table 6 or 7, keyed on the building's own full value. It prices only at deductibles of 750,000 or 1,000,000 and above the 500,000 NFIP maximum; a blank indicator prices $0.
  • Insurance to value above 100% prices $0; a limit $1 over value rounds to 100% and still prices ($1,639).
  • Floor and number of stories are statistics only; they never move the premium.
Silent $0, "rated": blank zone, blank deductible, blank excess indicator, blank IncludedInBlkt, ITV over 100%, deductible at or above total value. The engine matched chunk 25's hand model on every intermediate value in all 94 rated probes; the only refusal is an unfiled zone.

What to notice

  • Contents use their own "Contents" loss-cost row (OH Frame A: 2.988 against the building's 1.722) but read the building's construction. OH happy path $2,077.
  • Contents and building share one location flood value, but each looks up the table at its own insurance to value (83% vs 67%), so the factors differ (0.889 vs 0.991).
  • Excess contents must themselves exceed the 500,000 NFIP contents maximum: 400,000 of contents prices $0 while the building beside it prices.
  • In a blanket, contents take the blanket detail's deductible, not the location's (when the detail's contents flag is "Yes").
The policy total moves by exactly the flood premiums (base + building flood + contents flood) in every rated probe. Silent $0s are the building's list.
