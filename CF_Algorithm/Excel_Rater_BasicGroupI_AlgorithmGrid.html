Same five-stage shape both coverages share (loss cost → rate → cause-of-loss adjustment → insured's-choice adjustment → deductible), read side by side rather than as two separate lists. Rows shaded green are genuinely the same mechanism; rows shaded amber are where the two coverages actually diverge. Source: Excel_Rater_BasicGroupI.md §4, §5, §8.

⚠SpecificGroupIRate is a genuinely open question, not a filed lookup. No Set or Lookup rule populates it anywhere in either coverage's traced source doc — most likely a user-/schedule-entered value (industry convention for underwriter-assigned "specific" rates), but that's inference, not confirmed. Build as a user-entered column (Bldg_SpecificGroupIRate / PP_SpecificGroupIRate, separate per coverage) with a visible open-question comment, not a Rate_Tables lookup.

Reading the grid

SameGenuinely the same table, same key structure, same math — a difference in field names only (e.g. ...NonSprinkleredBldgRate vs. ...NonSprinkleredRate), not in what gets computed.
DiffersA real difference in the mechanism — extra gate, different table, different addend set, different multiplier count.
Contents onlyNo Building equivalent exists at all for this stage.

Five of nine comparable stages diverge in a real way (BaseRate's keys, Rate's addend set, Coinsurance's gate, LOIFactor's table and gate, FinalRate's multiplier count) — consistent with Excel_Rater_BasicGroupI.md §1's framing that Building and Contents "can't share a formula" for this form. AdjustedRate and DeductibleFactor are the two stages that are genuinely identical mechanisms, not just similarly-shaped ones.
