1. The headline finding — this is where Business Income stops copying Building's shape

Building's own Special building rate is a flat statewide constant with no construction dependency at all. Business Income's Special form is the opposite — it varies by construction type and by a theft/apartment-condominium classification that Building's Special form has no equivalent of whatsoever. This is the most structurally distinct of the five Business Income forms, not a smaller version of something Building already does.

Practical takeaway: don't template this form off Broad's simplicity or off Building's Special. It needs its own base-rate table (4-key lookup: state, construction type, coverage type, theft/apt-condo classification), its own theft-exclusion gate, and its own theft-exclusion factor table — three moving pieces Broad doesn't have and Building's own Special doesn't have in this shape either.
