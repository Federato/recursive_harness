Twenty-two chains, one shape, a lot of exceptions

Building, Personal Property, and Special Class each run four cause-of-loss chains — Basic Group I, Basic Group II, Broad, Special — that compute a rate unconditionally, then let a coverage gate decide which one actually produces premium. The two Business Income coverages run those same four plus a real fifth chain, Earthquake — and Earthquake's rate is charged through the per-group Earthquake and Volcanic Eruption endorsement records (CP 10 40 / 10 45 / 10 28 / 10 29), with Agreed Value adding only its increment (corrected 2026-10-02; chunk 10, C10-11). Special Class Business Income is the tightest-coupled of the five coverages: two of its five forms don't rate anything at all — they borrow the coinsured Special Class item's already-finished rate wholesale. Click any cell for its full decision tree: every gate, every branch, every terminal, cited back to the source rule.

Dashed amber-and-rust nodes mark a flagged finding — an empty rate table, an unresolved reference, a hard-coded value — logged in BUILD-LOG.md rather than guessed at. 2026-10-02: every open question on this page (Q1–Q7, the CA / AK District split, the Special Class symbol) has since been answered by the gated build; each note says how. What to notice

  • A real fifth rate chain — it computes a final rate unconditionally alongside the other four. Corrected 2026-10-02: the Business Income file has no earthquake premium of its own, but the separate Earthquake and Volcanic Eruption endorsement records for Business Income (CP 10 40, 10 45 sub-limit, 10 28 / 10 29 flat-dollar) charge FinalEQRate × limit; the build prices BI EQ $5 on chunk 10's case (C10-11).
  • Both premium branches charge only (AgreedValueFactor − 1) — the incremental load for waiving coinsurance — not the full rate. Nothing in Building or Personal Property uses this "surcharge on an already-rated base" shape.
  • Agreed Value adds only the increment; the earthquake premium itself comes from the endorsement record.

What to notice

  • Even tighter than Basic Group I's pass-through: this form borrows the Special Class item's fully-rated earthquake rate wholesale and applies no multiplier at all — only a gate deciding whether it counts or gets zeroed.
  • Strip away the six-step sub-limit endorsement machinery at the top and the actual rate-borrowing logic is a two-line copy-then-gate.
vs. plain Business Income. Plain BI's Earthquake rate is EQBaseRate × LCM × six of its own factors — a base-rate cross-reference plus real work. This form borrows the finished rate directly, no work at all beyond the gate.
