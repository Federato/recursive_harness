Twenty-two chains, one shape, a lot of exceptions

Building, Personal Property, and Special Class each run four cause-of-loss chains — Basic Group I, Basic Group II, Broad, Special — that compute a rate unconditionally, then let a coverage gate decide which one actually produces premium. The two Business Income coverages run those same four plus a real fifth chain, Earthquake — and Earthquake's rate is charged through the per-group Earthquake and Volcanic Eruption endorsement records (CP 10 40 / 10 45 / 10 28 / 10 29), with Agreed Value adding only its increment (corrected 2026-10-02; chunk 10, C10-11). Special Class Business Income is the tightest-coupled of the five coverages: two of its five forms don't rate anything at all — they borrow the coinsured Special Class item's already-finished rate wholesale. Click any cell for its full decision tree: every gate, every branch, every terminal, cited back to the source rule.

Dashed amber-and-rust nodes mark a flagged finding — an empty rate table, an unresolved reference, a hard-coded value — logged in BUILD-LOG.md rather than guessed at. 2026-10-02: every open question on this page (Q1–Q7, the CA / AK District split, the Special Class symbol) has since been answered by the gated build; each note says how. A dashed node now marks a real quirk of ISO's rules that the engine reproduces and ISO confirms, not an unknown.

What to notice

  • Simplest base rate of all twelve forms — a flat statewide constant, no class, construction, or territory dimension.
  • SpecialBuildingRate.RateTable.csv has no CW row filed — Special-form base rate is unresolved at countrywide level unless a state supplies its own row. Build: 45 of 47 jurisdictions file their own row; FL and PR override SetSpecialBaseRate to read residential / non-residential tables instead. ISO confirms the golden case $54 (OR) and FL $38 (CF-CHUNK3-ISO-CONFIRMATION.md).
  • Only form in the whole package whose premium rounding skips the nested-Round convention in favor of chained Products with no Round wrapper. Build: each nested Product carries DecimalPlaces="0", so it still rounds at every step; engine and ISO agree on every Special premium compared.
Q1 — resolved by the build. Intentional, not a gap in the package copy: SpecialBuildingRate is header-only at countrywide, 45 of 47 jurisdictions file their own rows, and the two that do not (FL, PR) override the rule. Swept 47 of 47 in chunk 3 and ISO-confirmed (CF-CHUNK3-GATE.md point 2).
