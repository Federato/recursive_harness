Commercial Property — account premium bracket

Two locations, three buildings, four occupancy classes, business income on two, TRIA accepted, every item written on the Special cause-of-loss form. Drawn at cause-of-loss form granularity: 36 leaf coverage records, of which 9 are gated off. Every box is a real datadef field from CFCW20260601V01.

The amounts are placeholders. Structure, field names and the arithmetic of the roll-up are real; no premium was computed.

Four coverage records per item — and they are not mutually exclusive

Building, Business Income and Contents each file four separate coverage records — Basic Group I, Basic Group II, Broad, Special — each a leaf with its own Premium, its own SetPremium and its own gate. The gates are the part that surprises:

RecordSetCoverageOnPolicyIndicator condition On when cause of loss is…
Basic Group ICauseOfLossToUse ≠ "Not Applicable" AND ≠ "" Basic, Broad and Special — any selection at all
Basic Group IICauseOfLossToUse ≠ "Not Applicable" AND ≠ "" Basic, Broad and Special — any selection at all
BroadCauseOfLossToUse = "Broad"Broad only
SpecialCauseOfLossToUse = "Special"Special only
A Special-form building therefore produces three premiums, not one: Basic Group I + Basic Group II + Special. Broad is the only record switched off. A Basic-form item produces two (BGI + BGII); a Broad-form item produces three (BGI + BGII + Broad).

The filed rates confirm the forms are incremental, not alternative. Alabama, class 0701 masonry: Basic Group I base rate 0.115, Basic Group II 0.080–0.156 by construction symbol, and the Special building rate a flat 0.039 — far too small to replace the other two, and exactly the shape of a top-up for the extra perils Special adds.

TRIA does not roll up like the others

CommercialPropertyPostTRIACoverage is a single policy-level leaf at depth 1. Its SetPremium is round(TotalStructurePremium + TotalPrsnlPropPremium + TotalBusnIncPremium + TotalSpecialPremium + TotalSpClBusnIncPremium), and each of those five is built on the same record by Sum(ForEach Location → ForEach item → that item's TerrorismPremium).

Those five are the only per-coverage account totals CF stores — and they total terrorism premium, not coverage premium. There is still no field holding the account's total building premium or total contents premium.
