Traced since, 2026-09-16. The "pick one family and actually read the rule file" step has now been done for the whole Special Class column and its Business Income sibling:

  • Special Class — core and Special Class Business Income — core: BGI, BGII, Broad and Special traced end to end. Earthquake not started. See Excel_Rater_SpecialClassBusinessIncome_BGI_HowItWorks.html, …_BGII_HowItWorks.html and Excel_Rater_SpecialClassBusinessIncome_ReuseMap.html.

All three turn out to file two separate endorsement records — one on the item, one on the Business Income group. Two are logged as accepted simplifications (S-1, S-2) in Rater_Simplifications_Backlog.md; theft is not yet logged.

What this inventory does not show, and should be read alongside. The columns tell you a family exists for a coverage; they do not tell you whether two columns share a table. Several families read the same matrix from different chains with different hardcoded keys — BroadFormBaseRate and SpecialFormIncldgTheftTimeElementBaseRate are both read by five or more chains at different rows. Treat a shared family name as a question, not an answer; the reuse map above is where that question gets settled.
