Building vs. Business Income, stage by stage

Business Income doesn't compute its own loss cost — it borrows Calc_Building_BGI.BaseRate and runs a shorter chain on top of it (3 steps vs. Building's 8). Rows shaded green are genuinely the same mechanism; rows shaded amber are where the two coverages actually diverge, including two stages Business Income collapses or drops outright. Source: Excel_Rater_BusinessIncome_BasicGroupI.md §1, §4, §5, §8, cross-checked against Excel_Rater_BasicGroupI.md §4–6.

Reading the grid

SameGenuinely the same mechanism — same table, same key structure, same math.
DiffersA real difference in the mechanism — borrowed vs. computed, extra multiplier, collapsed step, dropped stage, different branch count.
Business Income onlyNo Building equivalent exists at all for this stage.

Only one stage is a genuine match: COLAdj's shape (sprinkler/vandalism plus one form-specific factor). Every other comparable stage diverges — BaseRate is borrowed rather than computed, Rate carries one extra multiplier, and stage 3 is the real structural break: Building's three separate steps (coinsurance, LOI, deductible) collapse into Business Income's single Factor, with the deductible stage disappearing entirely rather than just changing shape.
