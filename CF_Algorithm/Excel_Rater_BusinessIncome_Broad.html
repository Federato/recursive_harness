1. The headline finding — there's almost nothing here

Every other Business Income form has at least a cause-of-loss adjustment, and Basic Group I/II add a coinsurance/type-of-risk Factor on top of that. Broad has neither. Confirmed by reading SetBroadRatesAndFactors directly: it calls exactly two sub-rules, SetBroadBaseRate and SetBroadRate, and FinalBroadRate is a literal Copy of BroadRate — no SetFactor call touches this form's final rate at all (confirmed earlier: Factor is Basic Group I/II only, computed once and never read by Broad's or Special's FinalRate step).

Practical takeaway: this is by far the easiest Business Income form to build. One lookup, one multiplication, done. The only thing worth double-checking before building it is ConstructionTypeToUse — see §2 — which is a copy from Building, same pattern as every other Building-owned key we've traced in this coverage.

4. The algorithm

1BroadBaseRate

if CauseOfLossToUse = "Broad": if BI_CovType = "Business Income Without Extra Expense": Lookup(BroadFormBaseRate: State, ConstructionTypeToUse, "Business Income Without Extra Expense") else: Lookup(BroadFormBaseRate: State, ConstructionTypeToUse, "All Other") → falls back to (CW, ...) if the state has no row else (CauseOfLossToUse ≠ "Broad"): 0.0

2BroadRate

if CauseOfLossToUse = "Broad": BroadBaseRate × LossCostMultiplier -- round to 3dp else: 0.0

3FinalBroadRate

FinalBroadRate = BroadRate -- literal Copy, confirmed line 8064 -- that's the entire final-rate step, no multiplier of any kind

5. Premium

-- gate differs in shape from Basic Group I/II's hardcoded-1 gate: CoverageOnPolicyIndicator = 1 if CauseOfLossToUse = "Broad" -- exact equality, not blank/Not-Applicable if BI_IncludedInBlkt = "No": ROUND(ROUND(IRPMFactor × PackageModFactor × FinalBroadRate × (BI_Limit / 100), 0), 0) × CyberIncidentExclusionFactorBroad × CyberIncidentExclusionCOLExcptnsFactorBusnIncomeBroadToUse elif BI_IncludedInBlkt = "Yes" and BlktTotalFullValueAmount > 0: -- blanket branch, out of scope, needs BlktBroadAvgRate else: 0.0

Structurally identical to Basic Group I's premium formula otherwise — same two-branch shape (scheduled / blanket), no Legal Liability carve-out, no Leasehold Interest branch. Only the datadef names and the gate's exact-equality test differ.
