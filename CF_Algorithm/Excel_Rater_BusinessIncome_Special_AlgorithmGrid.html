Reading the grid

SameGenuinely the same mechanism — same shape, same math, differing only in what feeds it.
DiffersA real difference — different table, different key shape, different term count, or a stage that exists on one side and not the other.
No equivalentThe stage doesn't exist at all for that side.

Only two stages genuinely match: SpecialRate's 2-term shape, and the premium gate (where, notably, it's Business Income's Special that matches its own Broad form rather than matching Building's Special). Everything else diverges, and two whole stages (insured's-choice adjustment, deductible) simply don't exist on the Business Income side — folded into nothing, not into a shared Factor the way Basic Group I/II handle it. Special is the only Business Income form with this shape: no Factor, but also no roof, no coinsurance, no LOI, no deductible of its own to replace it with. FinalSpecialRate really is just SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment, full stop.
