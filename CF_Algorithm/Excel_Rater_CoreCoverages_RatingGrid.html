Leasehold Interest does not fit this grid's four columns, and that is the finding. Its exposure is computed, not entered — a present-value annuity (AnnuityConstant × (1 − DiscFactormonths remaining), averaged between inception and expiration, summed across the four sub-coverages) driving the only <rul:Power> in the CF package. Basic Group I and II then Copy the parent building's BasicGroupICauseOfLossAdjustment; Broad and Special run their own lookups against LeaseholdInterestBroadSpecialRate and SpecialFormIncldgTheftLeaseHoldBaseRate. There is no coinsurance, LOI or deductible factor anywhere in it. Treat it as a sixth coverage with its own inventory rather than a fifth rating path.
"Completed" here means documented and built with calc-tab columns and example rows — for Specific/Tentative specifically, it also now includes the full factor-by-factor neutralization grid (which Class-rated factors drop to 0/1.0 vs. which carry through unchanged) worked out in the three companion build-process docs. It does not mean every open question inside that path is resolved — SpecificGroupIRate's own source (user-entered vs. some undiscovered lookup) is still unconfirmed for all three coverages, and Business Income additionally has an undocumented fourth RatingType branch. Those are analysis gaps inside a completed path, not reasons to downgrade the status.
