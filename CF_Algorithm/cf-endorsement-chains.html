Three ways an endorsement finds its rate

Each family attaches one record per coverage it can sit on, and auto-creates one cell per cause-of-loss form (Group I, Group II, Broad, Special, Earthquake). What differs is where the rate comes from. Debris Removal, Ordinance or Law and Green Upgrades are a slice or loading of the parent coverage's own rate. Utility Services and Unmanned Aircraft carry their own rate tables. Dependent Properties takes rates the submitter supplies for the dependent property. Every cell rolls into its record, then the parent coverage's total, then the policy.

All 223 rule files and their tables are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. The state changes cluster in Florida (which switches several of these off), Puerto Rico, New York, California, Virginia and West Virginia. Probes ran in OH, OR, IL (clean) and FL, NY, PR; every chunk 12 golden and zero case reproduces.

What to notice

  • The families that borrow the parent's rate inherit its exclusions: a vandalism exclusion moves Green Upgrades ($30 → $28) and Ordinance or Law, but not Debris Removal's flat Broad / Special 0.01.
  • Business Income versions are often a different coverage in all but name: Green Upgrades on BI never reads the Green Upgrades limit or percentage.
  • Several submitted values survive their rules (null guards): a debris minimum limit of 0, an Ordinance or Law factor, a Green Upgrades modification factor, a Dependent Properties rate.
No zero in these six families refuses after 542f0e1. C28-17 covers only building inputs. Blank limits, percentages, utility types, waiting periods, natures of dependency, operations and deductibles all price $0 (or the undiscounted rate, or negative) with outcome "rated". The only refusals are table misses that ISO also refuses: an unfiled Green Upgrades percentage, waiting period or nature of dependency.

What to notice

  • Debris Removal is a slice of the parent's own cause-of-loss adjustment (x 0.5, EQ x 0.75). The LOI factor comes from the parent's limit, never the debris limit (200,000 still gives 1.172).
  • Only the amount above the minimum is charged. A limit at the minimum is on at $0; below it, the premium is negative (OH −$29 / −$7, rolled into the building).
  • A "No Coverage" building still pays ($55 + $13) with no message; the "not valid" message lists only Legal Liability and Leasehold.
  • The Contents record is meant for a tenant whose building is "No Coverage"; otherwise ISO fires an error but still prices it ($67).
Submitted values are honoured: a minimum limit of 0 or a factor of 5 ($110 + $28; $642). State watch-items: FL minimum 10,000, Contents cells off, a sinkhole term on Group I / II; NY's Building EQ cell has no cyber factors. CW 2027-01 re-keys Group I LOI on CSPConstructionCode.

What to notice

  • Coverage A is a loading on the building's own final rate: 0.15 x rate x the full building limit (OH $53). Coverages B and C are priced like extra building limit.
  • A blank CovType charges Coverage A (the test is "not Coverage C Only"); B and C need an explicit choice. A blank post-loss option means "No".
  • A blank B or C limit isn't refused: the null limit is skipped and one $100 unit is priced, $0 at LCM 1 but $1 per cell at LCM 3.
  • CP 04 26's Coverage A reads the adjusted rate, and its B / C cells skip the deductible and multi-residential factors (OH B $74 against $72).
State watch-items: FL charges the whole Cov A factor (1.15, not 0.15), empties the post-loss factor (Yes = No), adds sinkhole to Group II and switches CP 04 26 off. CA prices public schools at $0 only with the Field Act exclusion; otherwise a No-Field-Act surcharge (factor 3 / 2). AK has its own minimum Coverage C; NY rewrites the nine EQ cells; VA adds a 43-file state broad form; WV's flood Ordinance or Law files are on the Flood page.

What to notice

  • It prices off the parent's own adjusted rate per cause of loss, so an exclusion that moves that rate flows through (vandalism: $30 → $28). Golden case: $30 + $3 = $33.
  • A blank percentage is the most expensive answer: ratio 0 selects factor 1.0, the undiscounted rate ($33 → $63). An unfiled percentage now refuses, as ISO does.
  • The Business Income version reads only the period of restoration and the BI record's own limit and rates; the Green Upgrades limit, percentage and related expenses are never read. A blank or 30-day period gives $0.
  • Its messages (no replacement cost, ratio over 1, wrong form, BI without a property Green Upgrades) are errors; the policy still rates.
V01 is not Florida's any more: FL now resolves to V04, while OR, GA, OH, PA, CA, MO, TX and NC resolved to V01 on 2025-01-01, where a submitted modification factor is recomputed. FL changes only the Group II cells (sinkhole, no cyber).
