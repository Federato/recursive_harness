A borrowed base rate, the Business Income factor, and one endorsement record

Neither Business Income coverage has an earthquake premium in its own file. The location's earthquake form (CP 10 40, 10 45, 10 28 or 10 29) decides which single endorsement record attaches, and that record charges the premium: final rate x the Business Income limit (or the sub-limit), then two cyber factors. Agreed Value adds only a 10% increment on top. A record attaches only when the coverage's own EQCovIndicator is a "Yes" value and the location's form names it.

Both chains start from a borrowed base rate, not a finished one. Business Income takes the building's filed earthquake loss cost and four building factors; Special Class Business Income takes the Special Class item's earthquake base rate x LCM. Both are then multiplied by the ordinary Business Income factor, so coinsurance, maximum period, monthly limit, type of risk and extra expense all move earthquake. The rules are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. Probes ran in UT and IN (Business Income) and OH (SC Business Income).

Not a finished rate
Base x LCM

The building's or item's earthquake deductible and coinsurance never reach Business Income: the building's deductible probes move it $72 / $42 / $0 while BI stays $420.

Not pure pass-through
x BI factor

Without a sub-limit form, the earthquake factor is a copy of the shared Business Income factor: SC Business Income moves from $2,500 to $8,500 across coinsurance and cover types.

Operational unit
Always $0

ISO's rule for the op-unit highest earthquake rate never writes its maximum, so that path always rates 0. The engine follows the XML; not checked against live ISO.

What to notice

  • Earthquake ignores the Business Income cause of loss, construction and the building's own earthquake election: Basic, Broad, Special and blank all give $420.
  • A sub-limit form (CP 10 45 / 10 29) swaps the Business Income factor for one based on the sub-limit against annual net income, and charges on the sub-limit.
  • No deductible, IRPM, package modifier or multi-premium credit applies to either endorsement premium.

What to notice

  • The rate starts from the building's filed earthquake loss cost (by class and territory) and four building factors, not the building's finished earthquake rate. The building's earthquake deductible and coinsurance never reach Business Income.
  • The Business Income factor does the rest: coinsurance, max period, monthly limit, type of risk and extra expense move earthquake ($420 → $580 / $870 / $730 / $540 / $380).
  • A sub-limit form charges on the sub-limit with a band factor: 600k of 1M gives $306, 400k of 1M gives $588 ("No Coinsurance" is dearer).
  • Agreed Value has an inner 3-place rounding (Indiana gives $10, not $9), and its gate is always true, so it also charges on top of a sub-limit form.
The operational-unit path is always $0. SetHighestBusnIncomeEQBaseRate (Location :1184) resets its working value to 0 and writes the maximum only if that value is null, which never happens; the Group I version compares and copies directly. What to notice

  • The copied rate is the item's earthquake base x LCM, written at the item's second step. With an item base of 0.5, SCBI uses 0.5 while the item's own finished rate is 0.352.
  • The item's earthquake coinsurance and deductible leave SCBI at $2,750; doubling the item's LCM doubles it ($5,500). SCBI's own LCM, class and territory are never read.
  • The rate is then multiplied by the Business Income factor; with a sub-limit record, by a sub-limit band x 0.999 (0.669, $2,010).
  • Chunk 10's $5 result only works from base x LCM: a finished-rate copy would give $3.
Nine silent $0 endings. Blank item earthquake class or location territory, blank SCBI limit, blank monthly limit or cover type, a sub-limit form with no annual net income or sub-limit inputs, a sub-limit over 125%, "Included in Blanket" with no earthquake blanket, and Agreed Value with a blank agreed-value limit all price $0 "rated". Refusals are table misses only: unfiled class or territory, a percentage deductible on a flat-dollar form, Seasonal Leases. State watch-items: NY and PR override the SCBI steps; FL overrides the final rates and Agreed Value and files its own factor; NV and UT at 2026-01 resolve CW V01, which overwrites a submitted rate; WA has no package in the corpus.
