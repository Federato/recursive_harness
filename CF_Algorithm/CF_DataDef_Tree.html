How to read a ../ path against this tree. Every parent–child step in ERC is really two hops — through a …Table wrapper and then into the row type. So from PersonalProperty, ../ is PersonalPropertyTable and ../../ is OccupClass. That is why the Contents rules reach the occupancy class with two dots, and why the policy-level factors sit five, seven or nine levels up depending on which branch you started in.
