Business Income Basic Group I — Factor decision tree

Every branch of the step-4 lookup, in order · traced against the rule XML, not just the source doc's prose

Source. SetTypeOfRisk* chain in CommercialPropertyBusinessIncomeRules.Rule.xml (SetBGIRatesAndFactors step 4, SetTypeOfRiskFactor line 5000, SetTypeRiskCombination1Factor/2Factor lines 4588+), cross-checked against Excel_Rater_BusinessIncome_BasicGroupI.md §4/§6.2/§9 (added 2026-09-11). Reflects the full six-branch version, not the four-branch version originally documented — two branches (Combined Type of Risk, plain Educational Institutions) were traced and confirmed filed, not left unsupported.

First match wins. The six branches below are checked top to bottom in exactly this order — a risk that satisfies more than one condition (e.g. BI_MaxPeriod="Y" and BI_TypeOfRisk="Educational Institutions") always takes the higher branch. Don't reorder these when building the workbook column.

if BI_MaxPeriod = "Y": → branch 1 elif BI_MonthlyLimitOfIndemnity <> "Not Applicable": → branch 2 elif BI_CovType = "Extra Expense Only": → branch 3 elif BI_TypeOfRisk is one of the four "Combined..." values: → branch 4 elif BI_TypeOfRisk = "Educational Institutions": → branch 5 else: → branch 6
1

Maximum Period of Indemnity elected Simple lookup

BI_MaxPeriod = "Y"

The insured chose to be covered for as long as rebuilding takes, up to a fixed number of months, instead of picking a dollar limit. Skip every other test — this always wins if elected.

Factor = Lookup(MaxPeriodOfIndemnityFactor: State, CovType, TypeOfRisk)
2

Monthly Limit of Indemnity elected Simple lookup

BI_MonthlyLimitOfIndemnity <> "Not Applicable"

A different alternate-limit structure — payout capped at a fraction of the total limit per month (1/3, 1/4, or 1/6), rather than one lump coverage amount.

Factor = Lookup(MonthlyLimitOfIndemnityFactor: State, CovType, TypeOfRisk, MonthlyLimitOfIndemnity)
3

Extra Expense Only coverage Simple lookup

BI_CovType = "Extra Expense Only"

Coverage for the cost of staying open, not lost income — priced off its own table, keyed on the loss-payment limit schedule the insured elected.

Factor = Lookup(ExtraExpenseFactor: State, ExtraExpenseLimitOnLossPayment)
4

Combined Type of Risk Blended — two lookups

BI_TypeOfRisk in ("Combined Manufacturing and Mercantile Operations", "Combined Manufacturing and Rental Operations", "Combined Mercantile and Rental Operations", "Combined Educational Institutes and Rental Operations")

Confirmed filed 2026-09-11 — not a missing table, just a second branch on top of the plain lookup. The underwriter enters a percentage split across two component risk types; the engine looks up BusnIncomeFactor for each component separately and blends by the entered percentages. No separate "combined" rate table exists — it's the same BusnIncomeFactor table branch 6 reads, just called twice.

5

Plain "Educational Institutions" Own formula — no BusnIncomeFactor

BI_TypeOfRisk = "Educational Institutions"

A correction to how this was originally documented: this is not just another row in the BusnIncomeFactor table reached via branch 6 — it's an entirely separate two-factor product. Confirmed by reading SetTypeOfRiskFactor's own branch directly.

Factor = Lookup(TuitionAndFeesFactor: State, ...) × Lookup(BusnIncomeEducationalInstitutionsExtOfRecoveryFactor: State, ...)

Note the asymmetry with branch 4's Educational component: the Combined-Educational blend uses only TuitionAndFeesFactor for its Educational half — it does not also multiply by the extension-of-recovery factor. Confirmed intentional, not an oversight to fix.

6

Everything else Simple lookup — the default

otherwise (Mercantile, Manufacturing, Mining, Rental Properties, Seasonal Leases with Monthly Limit)

The everyday case — no special limit structure, no special coverage type, no blended or Educational-specific handling. One straight lookup keyed on coinsurance percentage, coverage type, and business type.

Factor = Lookup(BusnIncomeFactor: State, CovType, TypeOfRisk, CoinsuranceToUse)
