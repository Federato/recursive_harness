Territory and symbol pick the rate; the state usually rewrites the rest

Basic Group II is the windstorm-and-hail part of a Commercial Property premium. Every coverage reads one table, BasicGroupIIRate, keyed by Group II territory and a construction symbol (B, AB, A, AA). Building derives the symbol from construction and number of stories. Special Class takes it as a submitted input. Personal Property, Business Income and Special Class Business Income borrow the keys of the building or item above them. A wind/hail exclusion switches every coverage to a separate "lowest rate" path.

The countrywide rules are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. The states are where Group II varies: 36 of 47 replace the rate rule with a switch on RatingTypeBasicGroupII, nearly every state adds dollar wind/hail deductibles, and TX, DC, NY, IL, FL, KS and PR rewrite larger pieces. Probes ran in ME (Building, Contents), OH (Special Class) and UT (Business Income), with PA and IN for the state rate switch.

What to notice

  • Territory is a lookup key in Group II, not a multiplier as in Group I. It keys the base rate and the BCEG factor.
  • Every wind/hail exclusion is the coverage's own record. Excluding wind on the building leaves Contents and Business Income unchanged; excluding it on a Special Class item also cuts its Business Income, through the class multiplier.
  • The countrywide chain ignores the rating type. Only the 36 state overrides read RatingTypeBasicGroupII.
What the engine now refuses. C28-17 refuses a missing Group II territory or BCEG, but only when a building's own Group II coverage is on at $0. A missing symbol or story count, a percentage wind/hail deductible below its floor, a blank state rating type, Limit 0 and a blanket with no total all still price $0 with outcome "rated", as ISO does. Unfiled territories, symbols, grades and wind/hail amounts refuse at the table lookup.

What to notice

  • The symbol is blank when construction type or number of stories is missing. Then the base rate, lowest rate and exclusion factor are all 0, and Group II prices $0 without a message.
  • The wind/hail-excluded path keeps both roof factors and drops the class numeric value and BCEG. With the exclusion, a missing BCEG or OpenSides changes nothing ($15 either way).
  • The deductible step has three branches. A percentage wind/hail deductible below its minimum insured total (1% on 90,000), or on a policy with no PolicyType, prices $0.
  • Countrywide Group II ignores the rating type: Specific and blank both price $48. In the 36 override states a blank RatingTypeBasicGroupII prices $0 (PA).
C28-17 refuses two zeros, only on Building cover types. A missing location territory or building BCEG refuses when the building's Group II is on at $0. A Legal Liability building with no BCEG still prices $0 silently, and so do a blank symbol, Limit 0, a blank deductible, a blanket total of 0 and the filed 0 numeric value for class 0580 in masonry. State watch-items: TX uses a flat 0.1 exclusion factor; DC and NY read no BCEG; NY takes a submitted symbol; IL maps "Cook County"; MT has its own symbol rules; FL, KS, RI, NC and AL replace the master chain.

What to notice

  • Contents decides almost nothing locally. A missing story count on the building zeroes Contents Group II too.
  • The wind/hail exclusion is the item's own record. Excluding the building leaves Contents at $34; excluding the item gives $11.
  • No roof factors, no multi-residential credit, no Builders Risk LOI branch and no LOI 0.0 branch.
  • The countrywide lowest-rate table is empty, but states file it: Maine prices an excluded item. The 8 jurisdictions with no such table all replace the cause-of-loss rule.
C28-17 has no Personal Property check. A missing territory or BCEG refuses only because the building's own Group II is also $0. Under a "No Coverage" building, the same missing BCEG prices Contents $0 and the policy is "rated". A blank building deductible refuses the policy at Group I's DeductibleStatCode lookup. In the 36 override states the Contents rate rule reads the building's RatingTypeBasicGroupII (read in the XML, not probed).
