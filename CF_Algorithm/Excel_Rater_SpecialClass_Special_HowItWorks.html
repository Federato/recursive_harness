How Special Class Special Form Rating Works

How Special Class Special Form Rating Works

Written: 2026-09-15. Source: CommercialPropertySpecialClassRules.Rule.xml and CommercialPropertySpecialClassSpecialCoverageRules.Rule.xml, read directly this session, plus a full 46-state sweep of both the rate tables and the state rule files. Companion to the Basic Group I, Basic Group II and Broad pages — this is Special Class's own Special form, standalone.

Status: traced, not built. On Special Class Worksheet, Basic Group I and Basic Group II are complete through premium and Broad runs to DeductibleFactorBroad. Special has no rows at all.

1. What's different about Special Class's Special form, in plain English

Nine steps — longer than Broad's five, shorter than Basic Group II's ten. Two things make it genuinely different from every other form in this coverage.

First, there is no countrywide rate. The SpecialBuildingRate table ships with a header row and no CW data row at all. Every other base rate in this coverage has a countrywide fallback; this one does not. If a state has not filed its own rate, the lookup returns nothing and the Special premium is zero. 45 of the 46 states do file one, and they vary enormously — 0.023 to 0.108, roughly a five-fold spread. This is the most state-dependent number in the whole coverage.

Second, theft is a dimension of its own. Special form is the only Special Class form where theft appears at all. It shows up twice: as a rate credit when theft is excluded, and as a second, separate deductible that can differ from the main one. Basic Group I, Basic Group II and Broad have no theft concept whatsoever.

Consequence worth understanding: because there is no CW row, a missing state filing produces a silent zero premium, not a fallback rate. That is a different failure mode from every other step in this coverage, where a miss lands on a countrywide default.

2. The rating chain, step by step

#StepWhat it does
1SpecialTheftExclusionIndicator Pure existence test: is there a CommercialPropertyTheftExclusionSpecialClass record on this item? That record is form CP 10 33 10 12 — Theft Exclusion, optional, attached at Special Class Detail level, so it is per item, not per policy.
2SpecialTheftExclusionFactor If CauseOfLossToUse = "Special" and the indicator is set: LookupSpecialBldgTheftExclusionFactor (State|CW + "Y"). Otherwise 1.0. CW value 0.88; 31 states file their own, mostly 0.94 or 0.91.
3SpecialBaseRate Two conditions, both required: CauseOfLossToUse = "Special" and PolicyType = "Commercial Property Policy". If either fails, 0.0. Otherwise LookupSpecialBuildingRate on State|CW + "Y" — no construction, no class, no territory, no protection class. One flat rate per state.
4SpecialRate SpecialBaseRate × LossCostMultiplier, 3 dp.
5SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment SpecialRate × SpecialTheftExclusionFactor. Note what is absent: unlike Basic Group II's COL adjustment, there is no cause-of-loss gate here at all — no "Fire" test, no zeroing branch. It is a plain product.
6AdjustedSpecialRate SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment × CoinsuranceFactor — the shared coinsurance result the item's Basic Group I chain already computed.
7SpecialDeductibleByLocationFactor Computed from the by-location record's TheftDeductible → 0.995 or 1.0. And then never used — see the callout below §3.
8DeductibleFactorSpecial A three-way Choose, and the only place in this coverage where a second deductible can drive the factor. Detailed in §3.
9FinalSpecialRate AdjustedSpecialRate × DeductibleByLocationFactor × DeductibleFactorSpecial — reading the shared DeductibleByLocationFactor (Basic Group I's), not the Special-specific one computed at step 7.

3. The full formula, end to end

SpecialTheftExclusionIndicator =
    1  if a CommercialPropertyTheftExclusionSpecialClass record exists (CP 10 33)
    0  otherwise

SpecialTheftExclusionFactor =
    LookupSpecialBldgTheftExclusionFactor(State|CW, "Y")     # CW 0.88
        if CauseOfLossToUse = "Special" and indicator is set
    else 1.0

SpecialBaseRate =
    LookupSpecialBuildingRate(State|CW, "Y")                 # NO CW ROW EXISTS
        if CauseOfLossToUse = "Special" and PolicyType = "Commercial Property Policy"
    else 0.0

SpecialRate =
    SpecialBaseRate × LossCostMultiplier                     (round to 3 dp)

SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment =
    SpecialRate × SpecialTheftExclusionFactor

AdjustedSpecialRate =
    SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment × CoinsuranceFactor         (round to 3 dp)

DeductibleFactorSpecial =
    # 1. a genuinely separate theft deductible, theft NOT excluded
    LookupDeductibleFactor(TheftDeductible,
                           SpecialIncludingTheftTotAmtInsuranceToUse,
                           "Other Cause Of Loss")
        if indicator = 0
           and TheftDeductible is not blank, not "Not Applicable", not "250"
           and TheftDeductible <> Deductible

    # 2. either deductible is 250
    elif Deductible = "250" or TheftDeductible = "250":
        LookupDeductible250Factor("Special")                 # CW 1.10

    # 3. ordinary case — main deductible
    elif Deductible is not "250" and not blank:
        LookupDeductibleFactor(Deductible,
                               SpecialTotAmtInsuranceToUse,
                               "Other Cause Of Loss")

    else 0.0

FinalSpecialRate =
    AdjustedSpecialRate × DeductibleByLocationFactor × DeductibleFactorSpecial
    (round to 3 dp)

Premium (scheduled, IncludedInBlkt <> "Yes") =
    round((Limit/100) × FinalSpecialRate × PackageModFactor
          × MultiPremiumAndDispersionCreditFactor × IRPMFactor, 0)
      × CyberIncidentExclusionFactorSpecial × CyberIncidentExclusionCOLExcptnsFactorSpecial

Premium (blanket, IncludedInBlkt = "Yes", zero if BlktTotalFullValueAmount = 0) =
    round((BlktLimit/100) × (FullSpecialClassValue/BlktTotalFullValueAmount) × BlktSpecialAvgRate
          × PackageModFactor × MultiPremiumAndDispersionCreditFactor × IRPMFactor, 0)
      × CyberIncidentExclusionFactorSpecial × CyberIncidentExclusionCOLExcptnsFactorSpecial
SpecialDeductibleByLocationFactor is computed and never consumed. SetSpecialRatesAndFactors calls it at step 7, it does its own lookup on the by-location TheftDeductible, and then SetFinalSpecialRate reads DeductibleByLocationFactor instead — Basic Group I's shared field. Confirmed by direct search: the name appears six times in this ruleset, all inside its own rule and the call to it, and zero times in the Special premium file. Either it is dead, or it is consumed by an endorsement ruleset outside this pass. Do not build it into the rate.

4. Premium

CommercialPropertySpecialClassSpecialCoverageRules.Rule.xml is structurally identical to the other three premium files: same two branches, BlktSpecialAvgRate and FinalSpecialRate substituted, and the cyber pair applied outside the rounded product. No state overrides the Special premium rule.

Special is the cyber outlier. Where Basic Group I, Basic Group II and Broad all share identical cyber values, Special does not:

FactorBGI / BGII / BroadSpecial
CP 10 75 attached0.9950.995 (same)
CP 10 76 — Full Limit, no aggregate1.0000.998
CP 10 76 — Full Limit, aggregate1.0000.997
CP 10 76 — Sublimit, no aggregate0.9980.996
CP 10 76 — Sublimit, aggregate0.9970.996

Special is the only form where CP 10 76 gives a credit even at full limit. Do not point Special's premium cells at the cells the other three forms share. New York overrides both cyber rules for Special the same way it does for the other forms — CP 10 75 credit killed, CP 10 76 becomes a flat 1.005 surcharge.

6. State deviations — swept, not assumed

Rate tables and all 46 state rule files compared against countrywide.

7. What a build would need

  • One new table: SpecialBuildingRate, 46 states, one rate each — plus Florida's SpecialBuildingRateNonResidential as a 46th row rather than a separate mechanism.
  • One new small table: SpecialBldgTheftExclusionFactor, 46 states.
  • One new input: a Theft Exclusion (CP 10 33) yes/no per item, and a Theft Deductible dropdown separate from the main deductible.
  • Two new premium cells: Special's own cyber pair — not the ones shared by the other three forms.
  • No new deductible pull: Special shares Broad's "Other Cause Of Loss" column, already extracted.
  • Reuse as-is: coinsurance, by-location, loss cost multiplier — all already built for Basic Group I.
