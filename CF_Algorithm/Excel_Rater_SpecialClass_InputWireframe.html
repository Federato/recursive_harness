Business Income — a child of the item, not more rows on it

Special Class Business Income is its own datadef group with its own Basic Group I / Basic Group II / Broad / Special / Earthquake chains. It does not compute a rate — step 1 of its Basic Group I chain is a straight Copy of the parent item's BasicGroupIRate. Everything below exists to produce one factor that multiplies that inherited rate. So this belongs as a separate block keyed to the parent item, not as extra rows in the table above — one Special Class item can carry Business Income or not.

⚠The single most important thing about these inputs: Maximum Period of Indemnity, Monthly Limit of Indemnity, Extra Expense limits and Type of Risk look like four factors that multiply together. They do not. SetFactor is a four-way Choose that picks exactly one, first match wins, and discards the other three. That is why the rows below are ordered as they are — the order is the precedence.

What each Cause of Loss selection actually turns on

The "Cause of Loss" field isn't a label — it's the gate. Basic is the odd one: it fires both Basic Group I and Basic Group II simultaneously (both premium files hard-code their coverage-on-policy indicator to 1), where Broad and Special each require an exact match and fire alone.

Cause of Loss valueFiresGate mechanism
BasicBGI + BGII, togetherBoth premium files hard-code CoverageOnPolicyIndicator = 1 — always on, not gated by this selection at all; cause-of-loss enforcement happens by which coverage record exists upstream
BroadBROAD onlyExact match: CauseOfLossToUse = "Broad"
SpecialSPECIAL onlyExact match: CauseOfLossToUse = "Special"
⚠This means "Basic" items need both BGI's and BGII's inputs filled in at once — Protection Class and Vandalism Exclusion for the BGI side, BGII Territory and the Construction Symbol open question for the BGII side. A UI that only shows BGI's fields when "Basic" is selected would silently leave Basic Group II's chain unable to compute.
