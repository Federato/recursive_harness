A building under construction, rated on its own or through a reporting form

A Builders Risk building has CovType "Builders Risk" and class 1150. Without a reporting form it rates like any building, with class 1150 picking its own rows (coinsurance 1.0, Broad 0.012, Special 0.015, a Builders Risk earthquake factor). With a reporting form (CP 11 05), the building's limit should be 0: the form carries the limit and five coverage records, each priced at the building's own rate x 1.82, and the building's own Group I / II / Broad / Special records stay on at $0. The premium adjustment form (CP 11 06) then re-prices each reporting period at its new value and charges only the increase, pro-rated by days.

Renovations rate an addition to an existing building at the larger of a Builders Risk rate and a completed-building rate. The add-ons are small: two collapse options are a factor on the building's rate, the theft forms are flat rates, materials of others is a manual premium, and the sub-contractor forms carry no premium. Every Builders Risk rule is byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. Probes ran in OH (clean), FL, NY, OR and NV.

Earthquake
Charged twice

The building's own earthquake (CP 10 40) has no reporting-form gate. A building limit left beside a form charges earthquake on both (OH $4 + $4, NY $8 + $12).

Missing period
Whole premium

With no unit-1 period, or a blank unit number, the "previous premium" is empty and the whole annual premium is charged ($435, not $67).

Materials of others
Any building

Created on every building with no Builders Risk gate: "Yes" plus a manual premium prices on an ordinary building ($232), with no message.

What to notice

  • The reporting form copies the building's own adjusted rate; even the building's LOI is looked up at the form's limit.
  • The premium adjustment form is the only place in Commercial Property that pro-rates by a reporting period's dates.
  • Nothing in Builders Risk is ever returned: a period below the form's value prices $0, and only a period after expiry goes negative.
No Builders Risk zero refuses for being blank. C28-17's BCEG and deductible checks skip CovType "Builders Risk". A blank form limit, IncludedInBlkt, deductible, new value, renovation limit or CP 10 44 deductible all price $0 "rated". The one refusal is for the wrong reason: a blank Group II territory on a Builders Risk building with a reporting form, because the building's own Group II is always $0 there.

What to notice

  • Without a reporting form, a Builders Risk building rates like any other: OH, Special, 400,000: Group I $223 → $227 (2023 → 2026), Group II $114, Special $56, EQ $4.
  • With a reporting form, the building's Group I, II, Broad and Special stay on at $0; the form carries the money.
  • The building's earthquake has no such switch. A building limit left beside a form fires a warning, but earthquake is charged on both the building and the form.
  • A Builders Risk building with limit 0 and no reporting form prices $0 with only a message.
A refusal for the wrong reason. The C28-17 check sees the building's Group II record, which a reporting form always zeroes, so a missing Group II territory is refused even when the form's own Group II prices ($187). The BCEG and deductible checks skip Builders Risk entirely. The class 1150 branches, coinsurance skip and Builders Risk LOI branch match the cause-of-loss pages.

What to notice

  • Each period re-prices the building at its new value and charges only the increase; nothing is returned (300,000 below a 400,000 form gives $0).
  • A period that starts after the effective date is pro-rated by days (245 / 365 = 0.67 on a four-month-late start); a blank start date charges the whole increase.
  • A missing unit-1 period or a blank unit number leaves the "previous premium" empty, so the whole annual premium is charged ($435, not $67). This is the null-subtract behaviour (OI-115); not checked at ISO.
  • A period dated at expiry prices $0 (the engine prints "-0"); after expiry it prices negative (−$2 / −$1 / −$1).
OI-121's "other term lengths refused" branch can't be reached: ISO's default block always sets ExpDate = EffDate + 1 year, and the engine ignores a submitted six-month ExpDate. The covered-causes message fires on every period whose covered causes are blank. FL's own rule keeps cents on pro-rated periods (21.44); NY rewrites the form's Group II rate.

What to notice

  • Each group takes the larger of a Builders Risk rate (class 1150 x 1.36, or 1.82 with a form) and a completed-building rate (the building's class x 0.9 x 0.75); ties go to the Builders Risk rate. OR reproduces chunk 24's $454 / $89 / $24.
  • The two halves read different territory factors: the building's for the Builders Risk rate, the location's for the completed rate.
  • A blank rating type is not a zero: the completed Group I rate drops to 0 and Group I prices silently low (OH $642 → $220). A blank construction zeroes Group I, II and Broad, but not Special or EQ.
  • Off the five special classes, Group II always uses the Builders Risk rate; EQ needs the building's EQ endorsement, or it gets limit 0.
The building's construction still moves a dollar: Fire Resistive renovations give $267 on a Frame building and chunk 24's $270 only on a Fire Resistive one. State watch-items: renovation rates are rewritten in AK, AL, AR, CA, DC, FL, GA, IL, KS, KY, MT, NJ, NY, RI, TX and VT; NY's Group II is $0 unless the symbols are submitted on the renovation record itself ($276 with U / B).

What to notice

  • Only collapse options 1 and 2 use the building's rate: its Broad rate or Special cause-of-loss adjustment x (5 − 1). Option 3, theft and CP 10 44 are flat rates; materials of others is a manual premium; CP 11 14, 11 15 and 11 99 carry no premium.
  • Collapse prices on the building's limit to use, which on a Builders Risk building is the reporting form's limit. A blank option prices $0.
  • Materials of others has no Builders Risk gate: "Yes" plus a manual premium prices on any building ($232).
  • CP 10 44's Special deductible picks one of three factors (250 → 1.1, a filed value → the deductible table, N/A → the building's Special factor); blank is $0.
Florida is the big watch-item: its only package forces materials of others off, writes no CP 10 44 premium at all, and prices Builders Risk theft as the building's Group I rate x 0.25 ($2 instead of $5). AR, DC, NY and RI rewrite only the theft stat codes; no state rewrites the sub-contractor forms. Chunk 24's OR figures reproduce: collapse $8 / $187 / $235, theft $5, materials $232, CP 10 44 $3.
