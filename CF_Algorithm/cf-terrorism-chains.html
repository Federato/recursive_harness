Every coverage prices its own terrorism; the policy pays only the roll-up

Terrorism is not a coverage. It is an extra premium charged on every coverage the policy has, for losses from certified acts of terrorism, backed by the federal TRIA program. TRIAType decides everything: it attaches one family of forms (the in-effect disclosure with IL 09 52 / 53 / 86 / 87, the expiring cap forms IL 09 95 / 96 / 97, or the post-TRIA exclusions IL 00 30 / 31 / 32). If it is blank, nothing attaches and any submitted form is removed silently.

Each coverage then prices its own terrorism on its limit, not its premium: limit / 100 x a terrorism rate (loss cost by territory tier x the form's factor) x a few of the coverage's own factors. No coverage total includes that figure. The policy is charged through a roll-up record that sums them; in six states that roll-up is capped at 25% of the rest of the policy. The rules are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. Probes ran in OK, OH, VA, OR, NY, AZ, CT and FL.

Sub-limit
Of full value

The 5–100% test is against the full value, not the limit. A sub-limit above the limit but within full value prices more ($173 against $89), not $0.

Override
Charged whole

An override is prorated only on the estimated disclosure. Every other roll-up charges it in full ($500 where the rated premium was $63).

Silent zeros
None refuse

No terrorism input is in the C28-17 refusal list. A blank TRIAType, territory, full value or deductible prices terrorism $0 "rated".

What to notice

  • TerrorismIndicator changes nothing about the premium. "No" is still charged; without CP 00 70 it only raises an ISO error message, and our outcome stays "rated".
  • A coverage can show a terrorism premium the policy never charges: with an override but no roll-up record, the building shows $500 and the policy pays $0.
  • The cap cuts only the roll-up. Coverage premiums and the pre/post totals stay uncapped (VA: 1,689 / 804 under a $74 charge). NY caps only the in-effect and post-TRIA roll-ups.

What to notice

  • Days are counted from the effective date and divided by 365, rounded to two places. One day left rounds to 0.00 (all charged as expired); 364 days rounds to 1.00 (all in force).
  • A submitted ExpDate is overwritten to EffDate + 1 year, so a short-term policy cannot change the split.
  • A submitted TerrorismProratingProgYr3Factor is used as sent (the rule only fills it when blank).
  • The override is applied first on each coverage, even with TRIAType blank, and copied unrounded (123.45).
Three behaviours follow the XML but are not ISO-confirmed: the whole override charged under the calendar-year disclosure, the submitted proration factor being used, and the forced ExpDate. No live calls were made for this page.

What to notice

  • Terrorism is charged on the limit: r0(r4(r4(r4(Limit/100 x rate) x PC) x coins) x ded). The cause of loss is never read.
  • The sub-limit percentage is sub-limit ÷ full value, rounded before it is truncated. 150,000 on a 100,000 limit with 200,000 full value is 75%, charged on 150,000: $173. Above 100% of full value it is $0.
  • A blanketed building keeps the sub-limit factor but not the amount: its 300,000 blanket share x the 30% factor gives $681, against $268.
  • Under IL 09 87, a coverage without its own sub-limit prices $0 (with the "sub-limit is needed" message).
Silent $0 with no message: a blank full value under a sub-limit form, Limit 0, a blanketed building at full value 0, a blank deductible, IL 09 53 outside the fire-exception states, no territory, a blank TRIAType. State watch-items: the fire-exception states override all three exclusion factors (OR: IL 09 53 $36, IL 00 30 $36 where OK gives $0). States on CW 2023 V03 (CA, NC; OR before 2026-06) and V02 (MO) read the sub-limit as a percentage; under IL 09 87 the V03 building prices $247 where 2026 gives $0.

What to notice

  • Contents are priced exactly like the building, on their own limit, with their own protection class and coinsurance.
  • The deductible factor is the building's: blanking the contents' deductible changes nothing; blanking the building's makes the policy refuse.
  • The sub-limit is measured against "Full Property Value". The chunk 4 builder leaves it blank, so any sub-limit there prices $0 silently; with it entered, 150,000 on a 100,000 limit gives $175.
  • IL 09 87 is a policy form: when only the contents carry a sub-limit, the building beside them prices $0.
No terrorism zero refuses; a neighbouring input does. Silent $0: blank full value under a sub-limit form, Limit 0, blanketed contents at full value 0, no territory, IL 09 53 outside the fire states, blank TRIAType. A blank building deductible refuses the policy at the DeductibleStatCode lookup (C28-05). OR overrides the same three fire-exception rules.
