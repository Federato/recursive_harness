STEP 2 / 9

The eligibility gate

One test, reused three times — by the Heat rule, the Control rule, and the combine rule.

The credit only ever applies to twelve apartment-building class codes, and only when the insured actually elected it (dropdown ≠ No). Everything else in this walkthrough is downstream of this one test being true.

Gate, as Excel (build once, reference from every downstream formula)
' cell: a helper cell, e.g. C44 -- TRUE/FALSE
=AND(
  C42<>"No",
  OR(
    ClassCodeToUse="0311", ClassCodeToUse="0312", ClassCodeToUse="0313",
    ClassCodeToUse="0321", ClassCodeToUse="0322", ClassCodeToUse="0323",
    ClassCodeToUse="0331", ClassCodeToUse="0332", ClassCodeToUse="0333",
    ClassCodeToUse="0341", ClassCodeToUse="0342", ClassCodeToUse="0343"
  )
)
⚠The source rule repeats this exact test three times (once per rule) rather than computing it once. Building it as a single helper cell here is a simplification for the spreadsheet — the rating outcome is identical, since all three copies are byte-identical in the ISO source.

The empty table is a symptom, not the mechanism — the real disabling happens at the dropdown.

STEP 8 / 9

Wire it into FinalRate

One multiplication, appended to the existing chain on Calc_Building_BGI.

Before (Deductible Factor walkthrough, already built)
FinalRate = AdjustedRate × DeductibleByLocationFactor × DeductibleFactor
After (this factor appended, matching the source rule's own chain)
FinalRate = AdjustedRate × DeductibleByLocationFactor × DeductibleFactor
            × MultiResidentialPropSpecialCreditFactor  ' new — reads C47

On Calc_Building_BGI, the FinalRate cell's formula gets one more factor tacked onto the end, referencing BldgBGIRating!C47 for that row. No other step in the chain changes — this credit only ever touches the very last multiplication, per Excel_Rater_BasicGroupI.md §8.

⚠Building-only. Contents (Calc_Contents_BGI) has no equivalent input or factor — don't wire this into the Contents chain.

What this walkthrough doesn't cover

  1. Confirm the real column addresses on BldgBGIRating for C42/C43/BC:BH before building — everything here is placeholder layout, not verified against the live tab.
  2. The gate helper cell (C44) isn't part of the source rule's own structure — it's a spreadsheet-only simplification to avoid repeating the twelve-way class-code OR three times. Confirm that's acceptable before building, since it's a deviation from a literal rule-by-rule translation.
  3. This factor is Basic Group I Building-only. Basic Group II, Broad, and Special all also multiply MultiResidentialPropSpecialCreditFactor into their own FinalRate chains (per CauseOfLoss_Building_RatingAlgorithms.md) — this walkthrough builds it once for BGI; each other form's calc tab would need the same C42/C43/gate/table block repeated or shared, not yet designed here.
