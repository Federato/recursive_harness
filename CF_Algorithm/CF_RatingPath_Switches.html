Which selections change the rating path

Written: 2026-09-16. Source: a mechanical scan of every path-selecting rule — SetPremium, SetCoverageOnPolicyIndicator, Set*RatesAndFactors, Set*BaseRate, Set*Rate, Set*CauseOfLossAdjustment, SetFactor, Set*ExclFactor, Set*ToUse — in the core rule files for Building, Contents, Business Income, Special Class and Special Class Business Income, CW 20260601 V01. Every value below was read out of a <rul:Test>. Markdown companion: CF_RatingPath_Switches.md.

Reconciled against the built engine on 2026-10-02 (commit 630ba2f; chunks 1–27 gated). Confirmed against each chunk's gate file and ISO-checked golden cases, the rule XML re-read where a claim moved, and the three regressions (104_ --quick, 206_ --check, 157_ --all). Three claims were wrong and are corrected in place (marked Corrected 2026-10-02); build evidence is marked Build. Every path, and where the build stands lists every path and the chunk that built it.

The three kinds of switch

KindWhat it isExample
Enum valuea coded field on a record, chosen from a domain list CovType = "Legal Liability"
Electionan endorsement record exists or does not — a Yes/No, not a value CommercialPropertyTheftExclusion
Inherited valuecopied down from a parent; not selectable on the item itself Contents' CovForm, copied from the occupancy class
The third kind is the one that bites. A field can be tested on the item and still not be chosen there. Building the input next to the test is the natural mistake.

The big five

1 · PolicyType — and the three lists it rewrites

The widest-reaching switch in the line, tested in all five coverages, and it cascades:

  • The cause-of-loss list changes. CPP offers Basic / Broad / Special plus blanket; SPP offers fire combinations instead.
  • The CovForm list changes. DomainCovForm is keyed State + PolicyType + RatingType, and Legal Liability is offered on CPP only — absent from every SPP row.
  • StdPropPolGroupIFactor / StdPropPolGroupIIFactor exist only on an SPP — on the building, contents and Special Class (0.98 each, in the rate chain, not on the premium). Corrected 2026-10-02: this said “only on the building side”; the CW rule files for Structure, PersonalProperty and SpecialClass each set both factors (chunk 14, gate point 6). KS, NY and VT file no SPP factor, so an SPP there prices like a CPP.
  • Vandalism's gate changes shape. On a CPP it fires on an endorsement election; on an SPP it fires on a qualifying cause of loss and ignores the endorsement entirely.

2 · RatingType — Class / Specific / Tentative

Selects an entirely different base-rate source: a class-code lookup versus a user-supplied specific or tentative rate. Confirmed absent on Special Class and Special Class Business Income — RatingType, SpecificGroupIRate and TentativeRate appear zero times in either ruleset (chunk 26: a RatingType sent on a Special Class item is ignored). In the countrywide rules only Basic Group I has a Specific/Tentative path; Broad and Special are class-only everywhere.

Corrected 2026-10-02 — Group II is not class-only in the states. 36 states carry RatingTypeBasicGroupII; “Specific” prices Group II from a submitted SpecificGroupIIRate (blank → Group II $0 on building, contents and BI, silently).

Build, chunk 26 (gated 2026-10-02, ISO agreed on every premium):

  • RatingType is chosen on the building only. The occupancy class and the contents copy it (an inherited value: a submitted copy is overwritten).
  • Contents price on their OWN SpecificGroupIRate; with none they rate $0. BI uses the building's.
  • Specific / Tentative replace the whole class chain (base rate, territory, protection class drop out): building Group I $362 where Class gives $287. Tentative prices exactly as Specific and attaches the Tentative Rate form; no rule reads a TentativeRate field.
  • A blank RatingType prices at rate 1.0 ($1,204), at ISO too. FL, NY, WV and MO leave the roof ACV factor out of the Specific formula.
  • California's One-Term Loss Cost prices building and contents as Specific; BI falls to rate 1.0 — ISO does the same (OI-124).

3 · CovType — one field name, four value universes

The trap worth the most care. CovType is tested in four coverages and the legal values do not overlap. A single shared dropdown would be wrong in three of them.
CoverageValues tested in path rules
BuildingBuilding · Builders Risk · Improvements and Betterments · Condominium Association · Legal Liability · Leasehold Interest
ContentsValuable Papers and Records - Other Than Electronic Data
Business IncomeBusiness Income Without Extra Expense · Extra Expense Only · Not Applicable
SC Business Incomethe same three as Business Income

CovType is declared on Structure, OccupClass and PersonalProperty, and no rule assigns it on any of them — it is a submission input, entered per record. Two buildings at one location can therefore differ.

4 · CovForm — the Contents switch, chosen one level up

Contents does not branch on CovType. It branches on CovForm, and SetCovForm is a plain Copy ../../CovForm — which resolves to CommercialPropertyOccupClass. Chosen once per occupancy class; every contents item inherits it.

5 · CauseOfLossToUse — which form chains fire

Ten values tested. On a CPP: Broad, Special (Basic implicitly). On an SPP the fire combinations: Fire, Fire and Allied Lines, Fire and Vandalism, Fire and Sprinkler Leakage, Fire, Vandalism and Allied Lines, Fire, Sprinkler Leakage and Allied Lines, Fire, Vandalism and Sprinkler Leakage, plus Not Applicable.

Basic Group I and II rate regardless; Broad and Special rate only when the cause of loss names them. The value is read from BlktCauseOfLossValue when the item is blanketed and the item's own CauseOfLoss when it is not — so IncludedInBlkt silently changes where this value comes from.

Elections — 49 endorsement records tested in path rules

An election is not an enum. The rule asks whether a record exists, so a blank is not a neutral value — several missing-record defaults are 0.0, which zeroes a premium rather than declining a credit.

The four core exclusions each file a separate record per coverage — the pattern behind backlog items S-1, S-2 and S-3.

Worked example — how Legal Liability produces a different path

One enum value in, and what comes out is a different premium formula, two factors that exist nowhere else, and three things that silently stop applying.

SELECT               Building : CovType  = "Legal Liability"        (on the building item)
                     Contents : CovForm  = "Legal Liability Coverage Form"
                                           (on the OCCUPANCY CLASS - all contents inherit it)

RATE CHAIN           unchanged. Base rate, territory, protection class and the
                     cause-of-loss adjustment all run exactly as class-rated.

THE FORK             a Choose branch inside SetPremium - not in the rate chain.

                     class-rated : premium starts from FinalBasicGroupIRate
                     legal liab. : premium starts from BasicGroupICauseOfLossAdjustment
                                   ^ three steps earlier

WHAT STOPS APPLYING  coinsurance, the LOI factor, the deductible factors
                     (all computed upstream, then ignored)
                     MultiPremiumAndDispersionCreditFactor

WHAT STARTS APPLYING LegalLiabilityFactor          Bldg 0.25 / PersProp 0.50
                     AddlInsurableInterestFactor   Option1 1.25 · Option2 1.25
                                                   Option3 1.00 · Option4 ("None") 1.00

ALSO CHANGES         blanket: electing it ZEROES the coverage ($0 at the engine
                     and at ISO - chunk 8 gate; corrected 2026-10-02 from
                     "becomes unreachable")

PER FORM             BGI  -> BasicGroupICauseOfLossAdjustment

Build, chunk 8 (gate 9 of 9 after chunk 27). Golden case Building $60 / $7 / $4 / $12, Contents $132 / $15 / $11 / $93 (BGI / BGII / Broad / Special), all ISO-confirmed — Contents only once the building carries WindstormOrHailDeductible (ISO refuses without it; ours prices: OI-103, chunk 28). The Special-form split moves money: theft excluded, Contents falls $93 → $37 while Building stays $12. LegalLiabilityFactor's key is pinned by literal, so each coverage can reach only its own row. What this means for the input sheet

  1. CovType needs a different dropdown per coverage. One shared list is wrong — the four value universes do not overlap.
  2. Contents' form selector belongs at the occupancy class, not on the contents item. One dropdown drives every contents item beneath it.
  3. PolicyType must be set before anything else is selectable — it rewrites the cause-of-loss list, the CovForm list, and whether the Std Prop Pol factors exist at all.
  4. Elections are Yes/No records, not enum values. A blank is not neutral.
  5. Each coverage needs its own election column for theft, vandalism, wind/hail and sprinkler leakage. Sharing one input is the S-1/S-2/S-3 simplification, and it is logged as such.
  6. RatingType is a building input only (added 2026-10-02, chunk 26): the occupancy and contents copy it. Contents still need their own SpecificGroupIRate when the building is Specific.
  7. Some switches only work with a companion input (added 2026-10-02): CA Legal Liability needs the location's District; Contents at ISO need the building's WindstormOrHailDeductible; NY needs a submitted Group II symbol; Flood needs its General Information record. Without them the engine prices (often $0) where ISO refuses or also prices $0.
