Two coverages look up a Group I rate; the other three borrow one

Basic Group I is the fire-and-lightning part of every Commercial Property premium. Building and Special Class each look up their own base rate: Building by class and construction, Special Class by a named class and a protection-class band. Personal Property looks up its own rate too, but it takes the rating type, class code, construction and deductible from the building. Business Income and Special Class Business Income have no Group I rate table at all. They start from their parent's rate and multiply by one Business Income factor.

Every Basic Group I rule file and every countrywide table these chains read is byte-identical between CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. Each probe gave the same premium at both editions, so one chain per coverage covers both. Some states override pieces: AL, CA, AK, FL, NJ, DC and PR are noted where they matter.

Editions
No change

All ten Basic Group I rule files and their countrywide tables are byte-identical in CW 2023 V04 and CW 2026-06. Premiums move only through state data.

Silent $0 paths
Still "rated"

Commit 542f0e1 refuses silent zeros on building inputs only. For Group I that is Arizona's missing reduction credit. Every other zero ending prices $0 without a message, as ISO does.

Biggest correction
Base x LCM

Special Class Business Income copies the item's base rate x LCM, not its finished rate. The item's coinsurance and deductible never reach it.

What to notice

  • Rate the building first. Contents and Business Income read values the building chain has already written.
  • The Business Income coverages have no coinsurance, LOI or deductible step of their own. One factor replaces them, and coinsurance is priced inside it.
  • Special Class and both Business Income coverages are always on. Their Basic Group I record is created for every item, whatever the cause of loss.
No referral fires on a zero. A missing class code, construction, territory, deductible or blanket match prices Group I at $0 with outcome "rated". The engine refuses only where a lookup that must return one row finds none, as ISO does.

What to notice

  • The rating type only picks the starting rate. Class, Specific, Tentative and a blank type all go on through cause of loss, coinsurance, LOI and deductible.
  • A blank rating type is not a zero. It sets the rate to 1.0 and prices high: $1,091 against $720 on the Oklahoma probe policy.
  • Legal Liability skips coinsurance, LOI, deductible and the multi-premium credit. It multiplies straight off the cause-of-loss rate.
  • LOI and deductible each have a 0.0 branch: no limit or no construction zeroes LOI, and a blank deductible zeroes the deductible factor.
Silent $0 paths. No class code, no construction, no territory, a blank deductible, a blank IncludedInBlkt, a Leasehold item or an unmatched blanket ID all price $0 with outcome "rated". C28-17 adds one Group I refusal: Arizona without BasicGroupIReductionCredit. An unknown class or an unfiled construction type refuses at the table lookup, as ISO does. State watch-item: CA and AK rate from District I / II tables; CA without District still prices $0 silently (chunk 27 C27-06).

What to notice

  • The item does not choose its own rating type or class. Both come from the building, and values typed on the item are overwritten.
  • Contents has a real Specific/Tentative path. A Specific building gives Specific contents ($253 in the probe), not $0.
  • A missing protection class zeroes Contents but not Building: Building uses 1.0 there and prices $751.
  • The forced-100% coinsurance branch never reaches this premium. The same conditions switch the coverage off, and the reporting-form rules charge it.
More silent zeros than Building, and C28-17 checks none of them. No building class code, construction or territory, no location protection class, class 1150, or a Yard item with no construction all price Contents $0 with outcome "rated". In three of these the whole policy totals $0 and is still "rated". One change goes the other way: a blank building deductible now refuses the policy at the DeductibleStatCode lookup (C28-05). It no longer gives Contents a silent $0.

What to notice

  • Two lookups in a row make the base rate: class description to option code, then option code and protection-class band to the rate. Construction plays no part.
  • The band can't really be blank. The location writes "1 to 4", "5 to 8" or "9 to 10", and a missing ProtectClass becomes "9 to 10". In practice the base-rate gate tests only the class description.
  • No LOI factor, no sprinkler term, no territory and no multi-residential credit anywhere in the chain.
  • The premium is rounded to whole dollars after every multiplication, and two cyber factors apply. Package mod 0.75 with IRPM 0.99 gives $50, where one rounding would give $49.
Silent $0 paths. A blank deductible, a blank class description, or a blanketed item with no policy blanket record prices Group I at $0 with outcome "rated". A class description spelled the way another state files it (Florida's spelling sent in Ohio) refuses at the option lookup, as ISO does.
