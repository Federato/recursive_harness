Blanket Rating Chains

How Commercial Property blanket rating works: the policy's blanket records (each Detail's total value, average or highest rate per form, coinsurance, builders risk and margin clause), how each coverage joins a blanket and what it copies back, and the blanket premium formula for every coverage and form. Checked against the built engine and ISO's rule XML as of 2026-10-06. CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06 are drawn together because their blanket rules are byte-identical; CW 2023 V01–V03, still used by some states, are noted.

One Detail record sets the rate for everything with its ID

A blanket insures several items under one limit. The policy needs BlktRated = "Yes" and MUBlktRated = "No" (blank defaults to "No"); each blanket is a Detail record with a UnitNumber. Every building, contents, special class and BI item that says IncludedInBlkt = "Yes" with a matching BlktIDNum joins it. The Detail adds up the members' full values, builds one average (or highest) rate per form, and applies its own coinsurance (0.95, unless 90% or N/A) and builders-risk (1.05) factors. The members copy back the Detail's cause of loss, coinsurance, total, limit and rates.

Each member is then charged blanket limit / 100 x its share of the total x the average rate, with its own premium modifiers. The blanket rules are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06; CW 2023 V01–V03 use a 2-decimal BlktProportion instead of the exact share. Probes ran in OK, OH, OR and IN; chunk 9's golden case still gives $255 / $510.

A member drops out
Limit moves

If one member's full value is blank or its ID doesn't match, the total shrinks but the limit doesn't: the other member is charged the whole blanket ($1,881 instead of $705), with no message.

Blank IncludedInBlkt
$0, "rated"

Building, Contents, BI and SCBI price $0 with the coverage still on. Special Class alone treats blank as scheduled.

The Detail wins
Its cause of loss

A blanketed member's own cause of loss and coinsurance are ignored: a Special building in a Broad blanket prices Broad, and its Special is off.

What to notice

  • The Detail's Type doesn't filter members: a "Building Only" blanket with a contents item still averages the contents in.
  • The Detail's total never falls below 1, so the premium's "total > 0" guard only catches a failed join.
  • Submitted values beat computed ones: a Detail average rate (0.5 → $500 / $1,000) or a member BlktLimit, which also moves the shared average for every other member.

What to notice

  • Average weights each member by full value (whole-dollar products, divide, round to 3 places); Highest takes the single worst member. OK: Average 0.705, Highest 0.758.
  • The member's LOI size changes too: its share of the blanket limit under Average, its own full value under Highest.
  • Other forms use the same shape: Group II, Broad and Special gate on the Detail's cause of loss; Special Class has no LOI or multi-residential factor; BI multiplies its Factor.
  • Margin clause 110% gives 0.94; the margin-clause and EQ sub-limit blanket rule files carry no rating logic of their own.
A member dropping out over-charges the rest. With one of two buildings missing its full value (no message) or unmatched (a message), the remaining building takes the whole $300,000 limit at its own rate: $1,881 instead of $705.

What to notice

  • The blanket's cause of loss replaces the item's: a Special building in a Broad blanket prices Broad, and Broad buildings in a Basic blanket get no Broad premium.
  • A blank IncludedInBlkt is a silent $0 on both, with the coverage on. An unmatched ID turns Building off but leaves Contents Group I / II on at $0.
  • Contents Basic Group II truncates the blanket limit / 100; every other form divides exactly (limit 301,199: $128 against $129).
  • Only Building has a blanket coinsurance branch (0.95); Contents' own factor feeds only its scheduled rate.

What to notice

  • A blank IncludedInBlkt prices $0 on every form with the coverage on: Group I / II send blank to the blanket side; SCBI Broad / Special catch it in "otherwise".
  • No multi-premium credit on either coverage, and no blanket LOI or coinsurance factor on the record.
  • BI Basic Group II rounds to the dollar at five steps; the other seven files round once before the two cyber factors.
  • A missing full value prices $0 with the coverage on.
