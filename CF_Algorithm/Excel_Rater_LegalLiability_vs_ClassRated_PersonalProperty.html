Legal Liability vs Class / Specific — Personal Property

Written: 2026-09-16. Source: CommercialPropertyPersonalPropertyRules.Rule.xml and CommercialPropertyPersonalPropertyPrsnlPropBasicGroupICoverageRules.Rule.xml (CW 20260601 V01), read directly. Read the Building page first if you have not — Excel_Rater_LegalLiability_vs_ClassRated_Building.html. This page is mostly about how Personal Property's version differs from Building's, not just from Class-rated.

Status: traced, not built. Legal Liability sits at "formula only" for Personal Property in Excel_Rater_CoreCoverages_RatingGrid.html.

1. The one-sentence version

Same shape as Building — the rate chain is shared and the fork is a Choose branch inside SetPremium that picks the rate up before the limit/coinsurance/deductible layer. But four things differ from Building's version, and every one of them is the kind that survives a copy-paste and fails quietly.

2. Where Building and Personal Property diverge

Eleven differences, every one of them survivable by copy-paste. Read this before building anything here from the Building page. The Building page carries the same table.

Which of the eleven actually move money. Given the same election on the same risk, only two change the number: #3, the 0.25 vs 0.50 factor, and #6, Special's rate source — Building drops the theft-exclusion credit and the roof-surfacing ACV factor, Contents keeps the theft credit. Both of #6's factors default to 1.0, so it bites only on an endorsed risk. #8 is equal by construction today (Contents seeds its local Limit from ../Limit) and is a latent risk rather than a live difference. The remaining eight are wiring: they change how you build it, not what it costs.
Identical on both sides. The two Legal Liability factors and their tables (AddlInsurableInterestFactor: Option1 1.25 · Option2 1.25 · Option3 1.0 · Option4 1.0, countrywide, no state filings) · no coinsurance, LOI or deductible factor · no MultiPremiumAndDispersionCreditFactor · rate side rounds to 3 dp and money side to 0 dp · cyber factors applied outside the rounding · a blank AddlInsurableIntOption forcing the factor to 0.0 and the premium to zero.
How Legal Liability actually gets selected — and it is not the same act on the two coverages.
  • Building: CovType is set on the building item itself. One item can be Legal Liability while the item beside it is Building or Builders Risk.
  • Contents: SetCovForm is a plain Copy of ../../CovForm, and ../../ from the Personal Property record resolves to CommercialPropertyOccupClass. The choice is made once at the occupancy class and every contents item beneath it inherits it — there is no per-item override, so Legal Liability and Building-and-Personal-Property contents cannot be mixed inside one occupancy class.
The list itself is DomainCovForm, keyed State + PolicyType + RatingType, six entries: Building and Personal Property · Condominium Association · Condominium Commercial Unit Owners · Legal Liability · Tobacco Sales Warehouse · Class Rated Building Only. Two constraints fall straight out of the keys: Legal Liability is offered on a Commercial Property Policy only (the Standard Property Policy rows do not include it), and it is available under Class, Specific and Tentative rating types — it is not confined to class-rated risks.

This is also why the additional-insurable-interest record sits at occupancy-class level on the Contents side (difference 5): it is at the same level as the form choice, which makes the pair consistent rather than arbitrary.

For the rater: a dropdown per building item drives Building; a single dropdown per occupancy class drives every contents item under it.

        x PackageModFactor
        x (Limit / 100)
        x round3( LegalLiabAddlInsurableIntFactor

Note the inner products round to 3 decimals while the outer ones round to 0 — the rate side is rounded as a rate, the money side as dollars. Building does the same thing with a different nesting order, so the two can disagree by a rounding step even where the inputs agree.

5. All four forms — and where each one picks the rate up

The Legal Liability branch exists in all four coverage files. The shape is the same every time — two Legal Liability factors, no MultiPremium credit, the limit and the policy factors on the outside — but the rate it reaches back for is not the same datadef in each.

FormClass-rated branch usesLegal Liability branch usesNote
Basic Group IFinalBasicGroupIRate BasicGroupICauseOfLossAdjustmentthe pattern
Basic Group IIFinalBasicGroupIIRate BasicGroupIICauseOfLossAdjustmentsame pattern, BGII datadefs
BroadFinalBroadRate BroadRate Not a cause-of-loss adjustment — Contents' Broad chain has none, so the Legal Liability branch reaches for the raw rate instead. Do not write BroadCauseOfLossAdjustment; it does not exist.
SpecialFinalSpecialRate SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment see the limit warning below
Special uses a different limit reference — and it is deliberate. Basic Group I, Basic Group II and Broad divide by [../Limit], the limit on the item. Special's Legal Liability branch divides by [Limit], with no ../ — the limit on the coverage node. That is not an inconsistency: the schema shows …PrsnlPropSpecialCoverage is the only one of the four that declares its own Limit (and LimitToUse); the other three declare neither, which is why they must climb. The Special file populates its own copy: SetLimit seeds Limit from ../Limit when null, and SetLimitToUse builds the adjusted figure the Class-rated branch uses — explicitly gated to exclude Legal Liability. So Special carries two limits on purpose: the raw one for Legal Liability, the adjusted one for everything else. Build both; do not collapse them into a single input cell.

The other three formulas

BASIC GROUP II      (CovForm = "Legal Liability Coverage Form")

Premium = IRPMFactor x PackageModFactor
        x (Limit / 100)
        x round3( LegalLiabAddlInsurableIntFactor
                  x round3( LegalLiabFactor x BasicGroupIICauseOfLossAdjustment ) )
        x CyberIncidentExclusionFactorBGII
        x CyberIncidentExclusionCOLExcptnsFactorBGII


BROAD               (CovForm = "Legal Liability Coverage Form")

Premium = IRPMFactor x PackageModFactor
        x (Limit / 100)
        x round3( LegalLiabAddlInsurableIntFactor
                  x round3( LegalLiabFactor x BroadRate ) )      <- raw rate, no COL adjustment
        x CyberIncidentExclusionFactorBroad
        x CyberIncidentExclusionCOLExcptnsFactorBroad


SPECIAL             (CovForm = "Legal Liability Coverage Form")

Premium = round3( round3( SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment x LegalLiabFactor )
                  x LegalLiabAddlInsurableIntFactor )
        x (Limit / 100)                                          <- local Limit, not ../Limit
        x PackageModFactor
        x IRPMFactor
        x CyberIncidentExclusionFactorSpecial
        x CyberIncidentExclusionCOLExcptnsFactorSpecial

All four drop MultiPremiumAndDispersionCreditFactor, which the Class-rated branch applies in every form. All four keep the same pair of Legal Liability factors, at the same 0.50 and 1.25 / 1.25 / 1.0 / 1.0.

Which forms actually fire

The Legal Liability premium branch tests only CovForm — it does not test cause of loss. The cause-of-loss gate lives one level up, in SetCoverageOnPolicyIndicator:

FormCoverage-on-policy gateEffect on a Legal Liability item
Basic Group Ino cause-of-loss testalways rates
Basic Group IIno cause-of-loss testalways rates
BroadCauseOfLossToUse = "Broad"only on a Broad item
SpecialCauseOfLossToUse = "Special"only on a Special item

So a Legal Liability item picks up Basic Group I and Basic Group II premiums always, plus one of Broad or Special depending on its cause of loss — the same stacking any Class-rated Contents item gets. All four gates also require no Additional Locations Special Coinsurance record and no Value Reporting Form record.

There is no Earthquake member of this set. The CommercialPropertyPersonalPropertyPrsnlProp*CoverageRules family has exactly four coverage files — Basic Group I, Basic Group II, Broad, Special. Earthquake exists elsewhere for Personal Property (Agreed Value, Inflation Guard and others), but not on this path, so there is no fifth Legal Liability formula to find.

Reading it against Building. Three of the four rows above are the same on both coverages. The two that are not: Special, where Building reaches for SpecialRate and drops the theft-exclusion credit while Contents reaches for SpecialCauseOfLossAdjustment and keeps it — and because Building's product also carries LimitationsOnCovForRoofSurfacingACVFactor, it drops that too. Both default to 1.0, so the gap only opens on an endorsed risk. And the cause-of-loss gate, which Building puts inside the premium branch and Contents puts in SetCoverageOnPolicyIndicator. Mirror one structure onto the other and you either double-gate or lose the gate.

8. The other two CovForms that skip the Class-rated branch

The Class-rated test excludes three CovForms, not one. Legal Liability is the one with a branch of its own; the other two are worth knowing about because they fall through the same gate:

9. What to build

  1. Share the rate chain through BasicGroupICauseOfLossAdjustment — point, don't rebuild.
  2. Branch at the premium row on CovForm, not CovType. This is the single most likely copy-paste error from the Building page.
  3. Use the PersProp row — 0.50, not Building's 0.25.
  4. Build all four forms, and check the rate datadef per form. Broad reaches for BroadRate, not a cause-of-loss adjustment — it has none.
  5. Give Special two limit cells. Limit (seeded from the item) for the Legal Liability branch, LimitToUse (adjusted) for the Class-rated branch. The other three forms have no local limit at all and read the item's.
  6. Put the additional-insurable-interest input at occupancy-class level, not item level, or accept and document the simplification the way S-1 and S-2 are documented in Rater_Simplifications_Backlog.md.
  7. Reproduce the 0.0 defaults, with a check cell.
