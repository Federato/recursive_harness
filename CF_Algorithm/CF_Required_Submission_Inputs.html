Chunk 28 — open behaviours, none built yet (added 2026-10-02)

Chunk 28 is open (2 of 22); every engine change needs the user's go. Until then a submission builder must guard against these itself.
  • Refusals (Set A). An unfiled or blank lookup key makes ISO refuse the policy; ours prices it, often at $0 — 66 live cases, 65 a SingleResult lookup with no row (docs/CF-CHUNK28-REFUSALS.md); also Convert of a blank (OI-99, OI-103, OI-117).
  • Silent zero-price inputs (Set B). Eight inputs still price $0 with no message (AZ reduction credit, unknown ConstructionType, no BCEG, no RatingTypeBasicGroupII, no Group II territory, blank Deductible on Broad and on Special, a construction type spelled as a rating type) plus NY's missing symbol; many are $0 at ISO too. A submitted IsNull-guarded field (e.g. SpecialBaseRate) overrides the filed table, at ISO too.
  • Minimum premium (Set E). ISO applies none; the decision is "refer to company" (OI-113). No referral layer yet.
  • Messages and form names (Sets C, D). Premiums agree; a few messages (OI-112, 114, 123, 125, 126) and 135 of 240 form names (OI-106) still differ from ISO.

Appendix — ambiguous, needs verification

Repeatedly treated as inputs in worked examples, but no chunk's docs ever stated "zero deriving rules" for these — not asserted into a category:

  • BasicGroupIRatingTerr / BasicGroupIIRatingTerr — used as lookup keys, weaker phrasing than the confirmed pure inputs.
  • Coinsurance (raw percentage) — appears throughout, never explicitly labeled.
  • FullBldgValue — a given in every worked example, never confirmed derivation-free.
  • TypeOfWatchmanSrvc — required alongside a confirmed pure input, not itself confirmed.
  • IRPMFactor / PackageModFactor — derivation status unknown. Resolved: computed only when blank, a supplied factor wins; endorsements take the policy's (chunks 14–15).
  • Special form's Deductible-removed case — unexplained. Resolved: the Special rule has an Otherwise 0.0 like Broad's.
