A small extra premium from one table, switched on by "Broad"

Broad is charged on top of Basic Group I and II, not instead of them: a Broad Business Income in Utah pays Group I $1,390, Group II $70 and Broad $230. Every coverage reads one countrywide table, BroadFormBaseRate, keyed by construction and a coverage-type row. Building uses Bldg / BldrRisk rows (0.015 / 0.010 / 0.012), Contents PersProp (0.021 / 0.011), Business Income "Without Extra Expense" or "All Other" (0.011 or 0.023). Special Class and its Business Income pin the construction to "Frame".

Each coverage's Broad record is created whatever the cause of loss and switched on only when the cause of loss is exactly "Broad". The rules and the table are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06, and only PR files its own Broad rates. Probes ran in ME and OK (Building, Contents), OH (Special Class) and UT and IN (Business Income).

What to notice

  • Six rules and no cause-of-loss step. Construction and class 1150 are the only risk inputs; a blank class still rates Broad at $16 while Group I prices $0.
  • The roof factor sits in the adjusted rate. At countrywide rates it rounds away; with LCM 3.0 it shows ($50 against $51).

The blank-deductible zero now refuses, in its plainest form only. C28-17 refuses it when the submitted CauseOfLoss is "Broad" and CovType is blank or "Building". Improvements and Betterments, Condominium Association and Builders Risk buildings, and a blanketed building whose own CauseOfLoss is blank (the check reads CauseOfLoss, not CauseOfLossToUse) still price $0 "rated".   • The base rate reads two literals, "Frame" and "Bldg". Construction, even blank or "Brick", and the item's cover type change nothing: every probe gives $14. Only a state table moves it (PR 0.011).

  • No wind/hail step. The deductible-by-location factor keys on the record's ordinary deductible.
  • The scheduled premium is rounded to whole dollars at each of six steps and applies the multi-premium credit.
