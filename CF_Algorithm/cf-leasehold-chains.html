A tenant's lease value, priced at the building's rate

Leasehold Interest insures what a tenant loses when the lease is cancelled by a covered loss. The record lives on a building whose CovType is "Leasehold Interest" on a Commercial Property Policy; anything else silently deletes it. It always creates 20 cells: four coverages x five forms. Each coverage turns its monthly amount into a net exposure, and each cell multiplies that exposure by a rate: the building's own Group I / II cause-of-loss adjustment, Leasehold's own Broad and Special tables, or the building's earthquake adjustment.

Only Tenant's Lease Interest is discounted: its monthly advantage is turned into a present value with an annuity factor chosen by the interest rate (5% to 15%). The other three are monthly amount x months remaining, averaged at inception and expiry. All 32 rule files and the six tables are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06; chunk 11's Oregon golden case still gives $268 / $30. Probes ran in OR, OH and FL, and in OR at 2027-01-01.

Factor files
Choose nothing

The 11 "Factors for N Percent" files only attach a printed page. The factor comes from two tables keyed on the interest-rate text; an off-table rate now refuses, as ISO does.

Special
$0 without theft

Special prices $0 unless a theft exclusion is on the building (the factor's else-branch is 0.0). ISO confirmed it for 2026; CW 2027-01 fixes it ($53).

Group II
$0 in 36 states

Group II reads the building's adjustment, so where RatingTypeBasicGroupII is required and blank, every Leasehold Group II cell is $0 (OH $86 / $91 and FL $138 when sent).

What to notice

  • A Group I / II cell is on whenever its coverage amount is not 0; Broad and Special also need the building's cause of loss; Earthquake is on unless "No Coverage" / "Not Applicable", including when blank.
  • Bonus Payments ignores the interest rate entirely, so a lease to 2099 makes it grow linearly (171,600); Tenant's Lease levels off at about 125 x the monthly amount at 10%.
  • The earthquake cell is the only one that skips the package modifier and IRPM.

What to notice

  • Golden case (OR, 10%, gross 1,500, 120 / 108 months): factors 77.0562 / 72.2213, net 111,958, Group I $268, Group II $30. All 20 intermediates match chunk 11's model.
  • Group I / II read the building's rate before coinsurance, LOI and deductible, so anything that zeroes the building's Group II rate zeroes all four Leasehold Group II cells.
  • A blank interest rate gives A = v = 0 and a $0 Tenant's Lease; an off-table rate (4%, 16%, 10.5%) refuses, as ISO does.
  • ExpDate is always EffDate + 1 year; months are non-zero only when the lease overlaps the policy.
Florida (its 2024-12 package on CW 2023 V04) overrides only the four Group II cells: no cyber factors, and a sinkhole term that can never apply to Leasehold Interest, so FL Group II = r0(r3(adj II) x net/100) x package x IRPM ($138). No other state files Leasehold rules (PR is out of scope).
