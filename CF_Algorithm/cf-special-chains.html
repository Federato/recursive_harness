State-filed rates, a theft dimension, charged on top of Groups I and II

Special adds to the Basic Group I and II premiums; it does not replace them. A Special building in Maine pays Group I $471, Group II $48 and Special $57. Building and Special Class read one flat state rate per state (the countrywide table is empty; 45 of 47 states file a row). Contents is the richest chain: occupancy category and risk severity pick the rate, the location's Special territory multiplies it, and two protective-safeguard credits can apply. The two Business Income coverages read a time-element table keyed by construction, extra expense and apartment status.

Theft runs through every chain: a theft exclusion multiplies the rate by a state factor (CW 0.88 for buildings; 29 states file their own), and Building, Contents and Special Class can carry a separate theft deductible. The rules are byte-identical in CW 2023-08 V04 and CW 2026-06. Probes ran in OK and ME (Building, Contents), OH (Special Class) and UT and IN (Business Income).

What to notice

  • Every theft exclusion is the coverage's own record. The building's exclusion does not touch Contents or Business Income; the item's does not touch SC Business Income.
  • The Business Income coverages ignore the Business Income factor, as in Broad: coinsurance, max period, monthly limit and operational unit leave Special unchanged.
  • A Business Income "apartment" is never an input: ISO derives it from the building's class code (0300–0343) and overwrites anything sent.
What the engine now refuses. C28-17 refuses a blank building Deductible on Special, but only for a submitted "Special" cause of loss on a plain building. Special Class's blank deductible or PolicyType, Contents' five blanks, Business Income's blank cover type and every blank IncludedInBlkt on the BI coverages still price $0 with outcome "rated", as ISO does. Unfiled theft deductibles, off-domain occupancy values and missing construction refuse at the table lookup.

What to notice

  • The base rate is one number per state. Construction, class and territory don't touch it (blank construction, class or territory all give $84); only class 1150 switches to the Builders Risk table ($18).
  • The theft exclusion and the theft deductible pull against each other. Excluding theft applies 0.88 and switches the separate theft deductible off. A theft deductible of 250 beats a building deductible of 1,000 and picks the 1.1 factor ($96).
  • Limit 0 gives LOI 0.0 here, not 1.0 as in Broad.
  • The deductible-by-location factor is Special's own: only a by-location theft deductible gives 0.995.
The blank-deductible zero refuses only in its plainest form. C28-17 refuses it for a submitted "Special" cause on a blank or "Building" cover type. Improvements and Betterments, Condominium and Builders Risk buildings, a blanketed building whose own cause of loss is blank, Limit 0 and a blanket at 0 still price $0 "rated". A blank deductible with a separate theft deductible prices Special at $77, while Group I goes to $0 unrefused. State watch-items: FL and PR override the base rate (PR rewrites most of the chain); AL, KS and RI override the rate step; WV the cause-of-loss step; 29 states file their own theft factor (ME 0.94).

What to notice

  • Three state-only tables set the rate: category + severity pick the base, the Special territory multiplies it, and the theft factor depends on category. All three are empty countrywide; Oklahoma files all three.
  • The deductible is computed locally but from the building's deductible and theft deductible. The item's own deductible changes nothing.
  • Each safeguard credit needs its type field as well as its factor, or it stays 1.0. Excluding Contents theft turns both credits off; the building's theft exclusion doesn't touch Contents.
  • The Alcoholic Beverages Tax Exclusion swaps the limit and also moves LOI and the deductible ($59, not $50).
Special Contents has the most silent zeros of any form, and C28-17 checks none. A blank occupancy category, a blank occupancy class (severity goes blank), a blank Special territory, Valuable Papers and a limit of 0 all price $0 with outcome "rated". Wrong values refuse instead. A blank building deductible refuses first, at Contents' stat-code lookup (C28-05), even under a non-Special building.

What to notice

  • The base rate is Building's state row: construction, cover type and an apartment indicator change nothing ($42). Ohio files 0.045, then 0.046.
