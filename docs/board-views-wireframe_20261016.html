Wireframe notes
  • Today's full grid stays one click away as the detail view, and clicking a cell still opens the same side panel.
  • The way-of-pricing view puts each column's outliers into words ("Out of date: NY · Checked by us only: PR"), so a nearly finished row doesn't need 51 cells.
  • "Not started anywhere" folds closed. It is the biggest group and the least to read.
  • The controls × states grid under today's board can take the same treatment: one line per input, with the states it has been rated in.
  • Commercial Property works the same way; General Liability is shown here because it is the larger board.
  • Drill-down: click a line (or a state, or a map tile) to open it; click a state chip or a coverage chip to see the requests behind that one cell. The side panel shows a breadcrumb back up at every level.
  • The request list is real for three sample cells (New York, Ohio, Puerto Rico on gross sales). Elsewhere it shows what the console would load.
