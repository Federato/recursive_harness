# The backlog, in release-candidate groups — 13 October 2026

**Written 2026-10-13**, after the 20261013 ZIPs shipped (commit `7307fc4`). It gathers what is open
across `docs/DEVIATIONS-PLAN.md` (section 8 and *Later*), `docs/deviations/FILINGS-LOG.md`,
`docs/OPEN-ITEMS.md` (the register, which stays the source of truth for each OI's detail) and the
August backlog (`BACKLOG-2026-08-14.md`, `BACKLOG-FEATURE-SETS.md`).

Grouped like the rater's release candidates: each group is a shippable step, and the order is the
order to do them in. **Needs** says what a task cannot start without: *your go* (an engine change),
*ISO calls* (counted against the daily cap), or something from outside.

**How it is ordered.** Records first, because the register still carries August items that later
work probably settled, and prioritising from a stale list wastes the next group. Then **wrong
numbers**, ahead of anything new: OI-127 to OI-132 are the only known GL defects that price a risk
wrong, stop it, or price it at $0 with no referral. Then breadth of evidence, then the next
deviations features, then what waits on someone else.
