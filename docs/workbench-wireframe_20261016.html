Wireframe notes
  • Four buttons become one live price. Every change reprices on our engine (free), so "Price it" goes away; the ISO side shows the stored answer or offers one call.

  • "Which states can take it?" and "Price it in every state" merge into one map. States where the risk is not legal are greyed, with the reason on hover. The response curve moves into the same panel as its third tab.

  • Commercial Property gets the same form, from the plan builder's Property catalog, instead of raw JSON. The JSON stays under "Edit the request directly" for specialists.
  • Mode and rounding move under "Engine settings"; ways of pricing walked and the request hash fold under the price.
