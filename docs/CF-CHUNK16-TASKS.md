re-rated them every premium matched. But those examples only cover part of each
family. **Of 43 premium records, ISO has priced 16 and never priced 27.** This
chunk proves the other 27 and the rest of each gate: every form, the zero

**What shapes the plan:**

1. **Every formula borrows a finished value from another record.** The Business
   Income options read their parent's `Final…Rate` (chunk 5 / 7); Pollutant
   Clean Up reads the *highest* rate across the location's buildings; Molten
   Material reads the building's Group I rate and loss-of-income factor.
   RC-1 resolves every borrowed path on a real tree and checks it is computed
   before it is read (rule order), the way C15-03 resolved the modifiers.
2. **The rates round twice.** Every Business Income option multiplies at 3
   decimals, then to whole dollars through the modifiers; Pollutant Clean Up
   and Molten Material chain several 3-decimal steps. Golden cases are chosen
   so the rounding points decide the dollar.
3. **Two shapes are chunk 15's.** Civil Authority's Radius form and three of
   Mine Subsidence's states (IN, OK, WV) are company-entered premiums, so they
   are cheap once the rest is proven.
4. **Mine Subsidence is five mechanisms, not one:** IL prices from tables
   (first $10,000 and each additional $10,000, by construction), KY from its
   own two tables, IN, OK and WV from a manual premium (OK through the policy
   modifiers). **The census's table count missed KY's, IN's and WV's tables**:
   they are filed under state names, not the countrywide ones (the standing
   check-sibling-tables rule).
5. **Editions matter twice** (the new per-edition check caught both): Missouri's
   2023 V02 has a different Business Income EQ formula for Extended Period of
   Indemnity, and no Special Class Molten Material files at all.
6. **Scope:** Molten Material's Vacancy Permit riders stay with Vacancy Permit
   (chunk 18); MA's heating-oil pollutant credit stays with the state-only
   chunk (23).
