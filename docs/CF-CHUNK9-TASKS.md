## What is structurally new, before any task runs

**1 · The census overturns its own opening premise.** The build order's own
description — *"Branch B is a stub"* — does not survive a direct read.
**Every one of the 20 `SetPremium` rules carries a complete, already-filed
blanket formula**: `IRPMFactor × PackageModFactor × Blkt<Form>AvgRate ×
(item value / BlktTotalFullValueAmount) × (BlktLimit/100)`. Building's
Special and all four Contents forms reach it through an explicit `Choose`
`When` on `IncludedInBlkt = "Yes"`; Building's other three forms reach the
same formula **by elimination** through their final `When`
(`CovType <> "Leasehold Interest"`); Business Income, Special Class and SC
Business Income reach it through a plain `Else` (their `SetPremium` is not a
`Choose` at all). **The one literal `Premium := 0.0` found by hand-reading
Building's Basic Group I `Otherwise` is not the blanket branch — it is the
Leasehold Interest exclusion.** Zero of 20 files stub the blanket formula out.
What's missing is not code. It's input.

**2 · The three-chunk finding (C6-28 / C7-25 / C8-14) is confirmed, and its
mechanism is now named.** `SetNonEQBlanketValues` — one copy per premium
group, five total, called from a per-group dispatcher that itself has **two
literal names** (`SetBlanketValuesSection` on Building and SC Business
Income, `SetBlanketValues` on Contents, Business Income and Special Class —
Chunk 8's shared-name trap, C8-05/C8-16, generalizing to blanket's own
machinery before any coverage-specific factor is reached) — is the **only**
rule in the corpus that ever assigns `BlktCauseOfLossValue` a real value.
It has three ways to leave the field short of that:

   | Gate | Condition | Result |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | `../../../../MUBlktRated <> "No"` (absent, null or "Yes") | **whole rule body skipped** — no `Else` at this level in any of the 5 copies |
   | 2 | `IncludedInBlkt <> "Yes"` on this item | field set to the literal **"Not Applicable"**, not blank |
   | 3 (inside gate 2's Then) | no `CommercialPropertyBlanketRatingDetail` row has `UnitNumber = BlktIDNum` | the temp field is never touched and keeps its initial **empty-string** constant |

   Every golden case that has ever produced the "empty `BlktCauseOfLossValue`"
   symptom hit gate 3 — `IncludedInBlkt` was set, but no submission has ever
   populated `CommercialPropertyBlanketRatingTable`. **The rule was never
   broken. Nothing has ever fed it.**

**3 · A wholly separate, 621,894-character, 135-rule engine already exists,
untouched by any chunk 1–8 script.** `CommercialPropertyBlanketRating`
(`ErcProcess`, 4 rules) `ForEach`es
`CommercialPropertyBlanketRatingDetailTable/CommercialPropertyBlanketRatingDetail`
and runs `CommercialPropertyBlanketRatingDetailRules.Rule.xml` — 32 `*Calc`
rules, 31 `SetMax*` rules and 30 `*AgreedVal` rules computing an average and a
highest rate per coverage-form across every risk sharing a `BlktIDNum`, plus
4 blanket-specific validation messages (`...Requires2OrMoreRisksToBeValid`,
`...RequiresAllRisksToHaveTheSameValueForEQSprinklerLeakageCoverage`, and two
more). This is what actually writes `Blkt<Form>AvgRate` and
`BlktTotalFullValueAmount` — the two inputs point 1's formula is starved of.
**Florida overrides this file.** No other chunk's census has ever opened it.

**4 · ISO's own fixture corpus has never demonstrated a blanket submission
either.** Of 924 STC payloads, 364 carry the `IncludedInBlkt` field (almost
always `"No"` boilerplate) and **zero** set it to `"Yes"` or carry any
`CommercialPropertyBlanketRating*` record. A chunk-9 golden case will be the
first populated blanket submission built anywhere in this project, and there
is no ISO fixture to check it against — OI-99's shape (ISO refuses/prices
where we don't) cannot even be asked here until a submission exists to send.

**5 · 12 blanket-only Rate Tables exist, all but one a single countrywide
row.** `BldgPrsnlPropBlktCoverageCode`, four `Blkt*RatingIdentificationCode`
variants, `BuildersRiskBlanketFactor` (1.05), `CoinsuranceFactorBlkt` (0.95),
two Earthquake blanket risk-ID tables and three Flood blanket tables. Five
states file their own `BlktSubStandardClassRatedRatingIdentificationCode`
(DC, IL, KY, NJ, NY); three (AK, FL, PR) file their own Flood variants. **None
of the 12 has been read by any chunk 1–8 script.**
