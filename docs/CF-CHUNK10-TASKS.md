## What is structurally new, before any task runs

**1 · The population count holds; two of the build order's other three
estimates do not.** `Rules/*Earthquake*` is exactly **97 files**, confirming
the build order's own count. But **Special Class is 27, not "roughly 29"**
(by filename and by content, identically), and **the Legal Liability count is
9, not 8** — one more than stated, and every one of the 9 is found by
*content* search, not filename: **zero of the 97 filenames contain
"LegalLiab" anywhere**, the same blind spot the build order itself
half-admits by calling them "endorsement variants" rather than naming files.
Non-Owned Trailers is confirmed to have exactly one file, both by name and by
content — the one estimate in the build order's sentence that was already
exact.

**2 · The real rating engine is not in the 97-file population the build
order points at — it lives in the same five parent files chunks 1–9 already
opened.** `SetEQBaseRate`, `SetEQRate`, `SetFinalEQRate`,
`SetEQCauseOfLossAdjustment`, `SetEQDeductibleFactor` and four more are all
declared inside `CommercialPropertyStructureRules.Rule.xml` and its four
Contents/Business Income/Special Class/SC Business Income siblings — none of
them named `*Earthquake*`, none of them in the 97-file count. Confirmed by
direct read, not inference: **none of the four per-form `SetPremium` files
chunk 1 built (`...BasicGroupI/II/Broad/SpecialCoverageRules.Rule.xml`)
reference any EQ field at all.** EQ's own `SetPremium` — inside the
`*Earthquake*`-named files — is a wholly separate premium node (its own
endorsement/coverage record) that *reads* the already-computed
`EQRate`/`FinalEQRate` and adds its own `Product ToDataDef="Premium"` on top.
**Two separate premium trees on one submission, not one tree with an EQ
branch** — the opposite shape from blanket, which chunk 9 found living
*inside* the 20 ordinary `SetPremium` files.

**3 · `EQClass` and `EQTerr` are pure submission inputs, the same shape as
chunk 2's `BasicGroupIISymbol`.** Neither field is ever assigned by a rule
anywhere in the countrywide corpus — confirmed by a corpus-wide
`ToDataDef` search, not just a check of one file. `EQTerr` *is* read (not
written) by `CommercialPropertyLocationRules` and by each of the 5 parent
files' `LookupEQLossCostBldg`-family rules, keyed `State + EQClass + EQTerr`
— a real, distinct territory concept, just not named `EQTerritory` and not
resolved by any county/place chain the way Building's own territory is.
"Its own EQ territory, EQ class" (the build order's phrase) is right about
the concept and wrong about the mechanism — there is no EQ territory
*resolver* to build, only two more submission fields to accept.

**4 · EQ shares zero multipliers with the ordinary chain.** `IRPMFactor`,
`PackageModFactor` and `MultiPremiumAndDispersionCreditFactor` are absent
from EQ's own `SetPremium` entirely — confirmed by direct read of the
Endorsement file's formula, which instead runs entirely on EQ-prefixed
factors (`EQDeductibleFactor`, `EQCoinsuranceFactor`,
`EQPercentageDeductibleFactor`, `EQDeductibleByLocationFactor`, and 13 more
sub-limit-tier variants — 57 distinct EQ-prefixed deductible/coinsurance
names total). This is the inverse of chunk 9's C9-19 finding (blanket reads
`IRPMFactor` at 3 different depths across the 5 groups) — EQ reads it at
**no** depth, in any group.

**5 · 66 EQ-named Rate Tables exist; 12 are read by zero `Lookup` rule
anywhere in the corpus**, including `EQRateBldg` and `EQRatePrsnlProp` — the
two tables whose names most look like "the" EQ rate table, and are not.
17 of the 66 are header-only at countrywide. State-filing patterns vary
widely even within one concept: `BCEGEarthquakeFactor` is filed by 41 of 47
states countrywide-absent-in-effect, while its near-namesake `BCEGEQFactor`
is filed by only 3 (FL, MO, SC) — a shared-name-adjacent trap (two tables,
one concept, wildly different state participation) not yet adjudicated.

**6 · ISO's own fixture corpus DOES demonstrate Earthquake — unlike
blanket.** 69 of 924 STC payloads elect `EQCovIndicator` to something other
than "No Coverage"/"Not Applicable" (all 69 sampled say
`"Yes, Not Included in Blanket"`), all 69 carry a populated `EQClass`, and
364 of 924 carry the field at all. This is the opposite of chunk 9's finding
— a real regression/confirmation set exists here from day one, spanning at
least 8 states sampled directly (UT, IA, IL, ME, PA, SD, WI, AL, MN) — so
chunk 10 does not face chunk 9's "no ISO fixture, ever" problem, and OI-99
(ISO refuses where the engine returns $0) is testable here in a way it
wasn't for blanket.
