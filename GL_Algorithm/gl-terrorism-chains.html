A premium on other premiums, switched by an answer and by which forms are present

Terrorism has no rate, ELP or loss cost of its own. Each group reads the finished premiums of sibling coverages and multiplies by an exposure-class factor. That makes it the last thing rated on a policy. Whether it rates at all depends on TerrorismCoverage and on the presence of two families of endorsement records: NBCR (CG 21 84 / 21 88 / 21 91) and the aggregate cap (CG 21 80 / 21 82 / 21 89 / 21 92). Exclusion forms (CG 21 73 / 75 / 87 / 90) only trigger messages.

All 51 jurisdictions are on pre-2027 today, and 46 move on 2027-04-01. Fifteen states rate terrorism by territory through their own factor tables, and FL, NY and AK override the premium rule itself.

Countrywide factors
.009 / .004

Above-average and average exposure class. The NBCR discount is 0.58. The 15 territory-rated states range from .004 to .133, and NY Manhattan from .038 to .098.

Precedence
Yes → NBCR → cap

The first match wins. A "Yes" policy is never discounted by the NBCR factor, and a declined policy carrying a cap form is still charged.

CW 2027
$1 floors

The combined subline is required, the cap ILF loses " CSL", and each group gets a floor. All Other Sublines' floor is unconditional.

What to notice

  • The exposure class is resolved per class code for Premises and Products (CW lists 105 and 54 above-average classes; LA, NJ and NY override). All Other Sublines is always Average.
  • The Endorsement group's factor is a user-entered field: CertifiedActsofTerrorismExposureClassFactor, with a lowercase "of". Its only bound is a message requiring 0 < factor ≤ 0.004 when no class is above-average.
  • The cap branch divides the terrorism ILF by the host's own FinalILF, so it reads a sibling's computed factor as well as its premium.
Messages, not switches. With "Yes", ISO messages that a certified-acts form must be attached, and that an exclusion form cannot be. With "No" and no records, it messages that an exclusion form must be attached. None of these changes the premium.

What to notice

  • The NBCR branch fires on CG 21 88 and 21 91 as well as 21 84. The manual reads 21 88 and 21 91 as outright exclusions, but ERC discounts all three by 0.58.
  • The cap branch rounds twice: the base product to whole dollars, then the ILF ratio to 3 decimals.
  • Pre-2027 also accepts a Premises/Operations-only subline. CW 2027 does not.

What to notice

  • The precedence and the three branches are unchanged. A declined policy with a cap form is still charged, and a "Yes" policy is still never discounted.
  • Floors differ by group: Premises, Products and Unmanned Aircraft need a non-zero base; Endorsement needs a non-zero endorsement premium; All Other Sublines has no test at all.
  • The cap ILF key drops " CSL", the same 2027 change that hits Premises and Products.
State overrides still apply. FL and NY keep only the "Yes" branch, so a declined policy with a cap form is not charged there. FL keeps the floor. AK's pre-2027 override does not discount CG 21 84. TX, VA and VT override All Other with four branches and no floor.
