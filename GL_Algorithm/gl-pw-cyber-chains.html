Five coverages that share rule names and no code, each priced off a host

None of these has a rate of its own. Product Withdrawal A and B multiply a small factor into the Products/Completed Operations loss cost or ELP, looked up again from Products' tables on a separate policy record whose subline is "Product Withdrawal". Limited Product Withdrawal is an endorsement on a Products classification and reads that classification's already-computed Products result. LoED and Cyber each rate twice per classification, once on the Premises host and once on the Products host, from the host's computed base rate.

The edition is picked the same way as every GL subline: all 51 jurisdictions are on pre-2027 today, and 46 move on 2027-04-01. Edition changes here are narrow: a new Product Withdrawal deductible table, $1 floors, a renamed participation field, subline tests on the LoED and Cyber gates, and bare (non-"CSL") ILF keys.

What to notice

  • Because LoED, Cyber and Limited PW read a host's computed values, they cannot be rated apart from the host, and a host's edition changes reach them without a rule of their own changing.
  • Three factor tables share the A/B/C table-letter axis: Expense 0.25 / 0.19 / 0.13, Liability 0.13 / 0.10 / 0.07, and Limited 0.20 / 0.15 / 0.10. The Limited table's name is misspelled in ERC: ProductWithdrawlFactor.
  • The Products/Completed Operations host for LoED and Cyber is rated even when Products itself is excluded, if the 2027 exclusion answer is missing. See CW 2027.
No ISO oracle for these premiums. The engine reproduces the companion doc's 2026-10-02 numbers and rates ISO's own Illinois Cyber and Missouri Limited PW samples. The full Product Withdrawal coverage has no ISO sample, so it was rated on a second policy record built from the rule file's own input fields.

What to notice

  • Product Withdrawal A/B have both a loss-cost and an ELP path, taken from Products' tables. Liquor and Railroad each have only one route to a base rate.
  • Pre-2027 Limited PW premium does not use the computed class premium when a participation percentage is given. It uses a supplied ManualPremium instead.
  • The two LoED/Cyber hosts compute their adjusted base rate differently: the Premises host rebuilds it from host components, and the Products host takes the finished Products base rate.
Engine limits, 2026-10-05. The engine cannot finish a pre-2027 Limited PW rating. It computes the class premium, then stops at the policy-level minimum rule. It also stops when a deductible spelling from the other edition reaches Product Withdrawal, and when an LoED limit falls in a refer band with no override. All three are refusals, not wrong numbers.

What to notice

  • The Product Withdrawal deductible keys change from "1,000 Per Occurrence" to "1,000", and the override is gone. A value in the other edition's spelling stops the engine either way.
  • Limited PW now uses the computed class premium and applies the discount only through the new ProductWithdrawalParticipationPercentageInt or a cut-off date. Its floor has no exposure test.
  • The LoED Premises host now requires the combined subline. The Cyber Premises host does not. The two Products hosts accept a missing exclusion answer as "not excluded".
New findings, 2026-10-05. (1) A pre-2027 submission sends the participation percentage in the old string field. CW 2027 ignores it, so the discount is lost: Missouri's sample rates 27 instead of 14. (2) With the exclusion answer missing, Illinois' sample rated Products at 0 but Cyber's Products host at 5, a premium built on an unbilled Products base. Neither raises a message or a referral.
