One rate build-up, two rule sets, and the date decides which

Premises/Operations has no edition switch of its own. The engine resolves the state package in force on the effective date, then the countrywide parent that package declares. That parent's copy of the Premises rule file is the chain that runs. Diffing the file across the five live countrywide editions gives exactly two shapes. Pre-2027 (CW 20231201 V02/V03, 20260101 V01, 20261001 V01) is one. CW 2027 (20270401 V01 and V02) is the other.

As of today every jurisdiction rates on the pre-2027 chain. On 2027-04-01, 46 move to CW 2027. California, Georgia, New Jersey, New York and Washington keep a pre-2027 parent.

What to notice

  • There is no hand-written calculator per edition. The engine interprets whichever countrywide rule XML the resolver hands it, so the edition dispatch is the resolver (gl_engine/resolve/resolver.py).
  • The payload carries no package version. A submission built against one version and rated without version= silently re-resolves to the newest state package, which may have a different parent (Kernel.rate docstring).
  • The 2027 ILF keys drop the " CSL" suffix. A payload still sending "1,000,000 CSL" misses every 2027 ILF row. The variant builder renormalizes it payload-side (OI-96/97).

What to notice

  • Medical payments is a separate, additive, 0-decimal charge. It fires only at the 10,000 limit and is built from the unrounded loss cost (or the ELP), not from BaseRate. On the OK case it is the $1 that turns 995 into 996.
  • The minimum premium is computed on the coverage, but SetPremium does not read it. It feeds the policy-level top-up, and every filed countrywide minimum is 0, so on filed data the top-up is 0.
  • AdditionalInterestFactor is read here by the two minimum-premium rules. It is not a premium multiplier, but it is not dead in this edition (E11 is CW 2027 only).
  • The deductible has a pre-adjustment stage that CW 2027 drops. Spray painting and ten named classes subtract the PD 250 Per Claim factor.
  • BasicLimitPremium (465) is a policy reporting figure. It is not the subline premium (996), and three earlier documents confused the two.
New finding, 2026-10-05. On a "Refer To Co." class (OK 54444), supplying the ELP override without the ILF-table override rates with FinalILF 0. The premium collapses to the med-pay charge: 8, against 5,358 with both overrides. ISO raises no Premises message for the missing ILF override. Strict mode prices it, and underwriting mode catches it only through R03.

What to notice

  • Med pay becomes a term inside FinalILF (+ factor − 1) rather than a separate charge. The algebra is close to pre-2027, but rounding lands elsewhere. On the 2025 golden inputs this chain gives 975 against pre-2027's 976.
  • The $1 floor is new. A risk whose rate rounds to 0 with exposure on the record now prices at 1. Pre-2027 leaves it at 0, and the 2027 manual does not mention the floor.
  • No minimum premium survives anywhere: not on the coverage, and not in the policy-level record.
  • Claims-made changes table and key, from ClassCode to ClassificationType. Year 7 on OK class 50017 still returns 0.92.
New finding, 2026-10-05, and worse here. Pre-2027, a missing ILF on a "Refer To Co." class zeroes FinalILF. In 2027 the med-pay fold leaves FinalILF at 0.007, above 0, so the clamp never fires. Supplying only the ELP override rates OK 54444 at 20 instead of 5,370. Without the ELP override the BaseRate is 0 and the $1 floor applies.
vs. pre-2027. Same rate sources and mostly the same rule names. The formulas differ at FinalILF (med pay folded in), SetPremium (no charge term, the floor, a stricter subline test) and the deductible (direct lookup). Fourteen rows in all: see Side by side.
