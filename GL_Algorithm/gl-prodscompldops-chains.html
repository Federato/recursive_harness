One base rate, two possible sources, and a published zero that picks between them

Products/Completed Operations rates from a loss cost when one is filed for the class, and from an expected loss potential (ELP) when the filed loss cost is 0. That 0 is a real, published value that switches the path, not a missing one. The class's ELP text selector says the same thing in words, in every row tested.

The edition is chosen the same way as Premises/Operations. The engine resolves the state package in force on the effective date, then its declared countrywide parent. The rule file has two shapes: pre-2027 (CW 20231201 V02/V03, 20260101 V01, 20261001 V01) and CW 2027 (20270401 V01 and V02). Every jurisdiction is on pre-2027 today. On 2027-04-01, 46 move to CW 2027, and CA, GA, NJ, NY and WA stay.

Pre-2027 chain
35 + 6

Coverage and premium steps. Split limits, the spray-painting deductible carve-out and a policy-level minimum premium are all still here.

CW 2027 chain
28 + 4

Those rules are gone. A new exclusion field decides whether Products is on the policy at all, and a $1 floor replaces a zero premium.

Unchanged across editions
FinalILF

FinalILF = CSLILF − deductible factor in both. Products has no medical-payments term, so it never gets the fold Premises picks up in 2027.

What to notice

  • The ELP text selector agrees with the "loss cost not 0" test in 620,856 of 620,856 rows across the corpus (GATE-336). Rule N17 proposed checking that at load time. The engine does not. It runs ISO's branch on the number alone.
  • Products has its own territory field. It is statewide 999 in all 51 jurisdictions, so in practice the loss cost does not vary by territory.
  • The ILF table assignment is a letter (A, B, C), not a number as in Premises, and no rule ever converts it. Its non-values are "Refer To Co." and "N/A", which means Products is not offered for the class (about 35% of rows).
Not offered is not modelled. GATE-336 called for a NOT_OFFERED outcome when the ELP text is "Not Applicable". ISO's rules have none, and neither does the engine's rating path. The class prices 0 pre-2027 and $1 under CW 2027, with no message or referral in either mode.

What to notice

  • SetBaseRate only computes when the coverage form is "Occurrence" or "Claims Made". An empty form falls to its Otherwise arm, so BaseRate is 0 and Products prices 0. ISO raises no message for it.
  • The Defense Within Limits factor is chosen by whether an endorsement row exists, not by a yes/no field. A model that treats it as a boolean never applies it.
  • The minimum premium is computed on the coverage but not read by SetPremium. It feeds the policy-level top-up, and every filed countrywide minimum is 0, so on filed data the top-up is 0.
  • The split-limit weights are Products' own, not Premises'. They are written on every quote in this edition, including CSL ones.
Silent zeros, 2026-10-05. Four inputs price Products at 0 with no Products message: an empty coverage form, a "Refer To Co." table with no override, an "N/A" class, and an ELP override supplied without an ILF-table override. Underwriting mode catches the two "Refer To Co." cases through R03. It catches neither the empty form nor the "N/A" class.
Engine layer. The purple nodes are the kernel, not ISO rules: the OI-94 null-loss-cost refusal (all modes), the total check, and the underwriting-mode referral register.

What to notice

  • The new ProdsCompldOpsExclusion field controls the combined subline. Products is on the policy only when the field is present and not "Yes". A blank or missing answer takes Products off silently, and no pre-2027 submission carries the field. The variant builder defaults it to "No" as a stopgap (OI-97).
  • The $1 floor applies when the premium rounds to 0, the class coverage is not "Included" and there is exposure. So an "N/A" class, an empty coverage form and a missing ILF all price at $1 rather than 0. The amount changes, but the problem is the same.
  • SetPremium no longer tests the subline. Whether Products rates is now decided upstream by the coverage-on-policy indicator.
  • FinalILF and BaseRate are byte-identical to pre-2027, so on an ordinary risk both editions give the same Products premium.
Silent outcomes, 2026-10-05. A missing exclusion answer prices Products at 0. An empty coverage form, an "N/A" class, and an ELP override without an ILF-table override each price at $1. None raises a Products message, and underwriting mode catches only the "Refer To Co." cases (R03).
vs. pre-2027. The rate build-up is unchanged. Rules were removed around it (split limits, deductible pre-adjustment, minimum premium), and two were added at the edges: the exclusion switch at the start and the $1 floor at the end.
